Domain-swap experiments and point mutations in the Nckl SH2 and SH3 domains suggest that specific Nckl domains mediate this proinflammatory effect. Experiments with the Nck1/Nck2 chimeras suggest that the Nck1 SH2 domain critically mediates these proinflammatory signals, whereas the Nck2 SH3 domains can mediate the proinflammatory response when coupled with the Nckl SH2 domain (Alfaidi et al., 2020a). Point mutations in the Nckl SH2 targeting the critical Arg residue in the SH2 binding pocket (R308M) verify that disturbed flow-induced proinflammatory signaling requires the Nck1 SH2 domain (Alfaidi et al., 2020a). While the Nckl and Nck2 SH3 domains appear to be redundant, inactivating point mutations in the Nckl SH3 domains targeting the critical WPY triad (SH3.1 W38K, SH3.2 W143K, SH3.3 W229K) suggest that the Nckl SH3.1 domain critically mediates flow-induced NF-KB activation and ICAM-1/VCAM-1 expression (Alfaidi et al., 2020a).
