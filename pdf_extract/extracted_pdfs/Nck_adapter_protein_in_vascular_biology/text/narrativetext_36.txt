The Nckl and Nck2 SH2 domains show both similarities and differences in their binding properties. The Nckl and Nck2 SH2 domains bind to a similar pY-D/E-x-V/P consensus sequence in target proteins (Figure 1A), although alterations in the C-terminal amino acids may affect binding specificity and affinity (Frese et al., 2006). Phosphorylation of Platelet Endothelial Cell Adhesion Molecule-1 (PECAM-1) on Tyr663 (pYTEV) supports binding to both the Nckl and Nck2 SH2 domain (Machida et al., 2007; Alfaidi et al., 2020b), whereas phosphorylation on the similar Tyr686 (pYSEV) site did not support binding of either Nck1 or Nck2 (Machida et al., 2007). Moreover, both Nck1 SH2 and Nck2 SH2 bind to platelet derived growth factor (PDGF) and epidermal growth factor (EGF) receptors, but the Nck2 SH2 domain binds with a higher affinity than the Nckl SH2 domain (Chen et al., 1998). This differential affinity may be due to Nckl SH2 domains and Nck2 SH2 domains binding at different sites, as the PDGF receptor phosphotyrosine residue pY751 specifically interacts with Nckl (Nishimura et al., 1993), whereas pY1009 specifically interacts with Nck2 (Chen et al., 2000).
