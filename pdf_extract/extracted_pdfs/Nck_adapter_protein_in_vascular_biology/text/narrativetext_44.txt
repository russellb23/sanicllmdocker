Proline-rich regions in proteins bind to a variety of domains, such as SH3 domains, WW domains, and EVH domains. SH3 domains represent one of the largest families of modular domains, with humans expressing over 300 SH3 domains in over 200 proteins (Mayer and Gupta, 1998; Teyra et al., 2017). Composed of ~60 amino acids, SH3 domains consist of five or six B-sheets arranged in two tightly packed anti-parallel B sheets forming a B-barrel (Figure 1D; Musacchio et al., 1992).
