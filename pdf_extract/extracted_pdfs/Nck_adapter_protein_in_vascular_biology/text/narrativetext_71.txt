FIGURE 2 | Proposed model for Nck1/2 regulation. (A) Intramolecular interactions between the Nck1/2 SH3.2 domain and a peptide in the linker between the SH3.1 and SH3.2 domains may limit SH3.2 interactions with other targets. (B,C) Phosphorylation of Ser85 in response to cell stimulation may reduce this intramolecular interaction to activate Nck1/2, allowing recruitment of effector proteins. (D) Tyrosine phosphorylation within the WPY motifs in the SH3 domains may limit SH3 domain interactions to inactivate Nek1/2.
