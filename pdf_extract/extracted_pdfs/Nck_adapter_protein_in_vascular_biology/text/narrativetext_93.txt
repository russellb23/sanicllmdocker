IREla phosphorylation promotes activation of JNK and NF-«B to drive proinflammatory gene expression (Cao and Kaufman, 2012). Nck interacts with proline-rich regions in IREla under normal conditions and represses ERK activation (Figure 3C). Activation of IREla in response to ER stress causes Nckl release and ERK activation to promote cell survival (Nguyen et al., 2004). Therefore, Nckl interactions with IREla and PERK serve to limit UPR signaling, and loss of Nckl enhances cell survival following ER stress (Yamani et al., 2014, 2015).
