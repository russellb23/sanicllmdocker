Figure 7. Nck1 knockdown disturb the phosphorylation of CREB and ATF1 LEC and shNck1 LEC were either stimulated with 30 ng/ml PDGF-BB or left untreated at 37 °C for the indicated times. Cell lysates were separated by SDS-PAGE and the western blot was probed with (A, B) anti-phospho-CREB and (A, C) anti-phospho-ATF1 antibodies. The quantified signal intensities are presented as a ratio of the protein of choice to the corresponding GAPDH values normalized to the value of unstimulated LEC. Data are representative of three experiments (mean + SEM). *p<0.05.
