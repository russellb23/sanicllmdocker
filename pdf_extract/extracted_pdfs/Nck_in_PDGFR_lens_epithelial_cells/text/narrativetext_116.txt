Figure 6. Nckl knockdown impaired PDGFR-mediated signaling pathway. LEC and shNck1 LEC were either stimulated with 30 ng/ml PDGF-BB or left untreated at 37 °C for the indicated times. Cell lysates were separated by SDS-PAGE and the western blot was probed with (A, B) anti-phospho-tyrosine (p-Tyr, 4G10), (A, C) anti-Nck1, (D, E) anti-phospho-Akt (pAkt) and (E, F) anti-phospho-Erk1/2 (pErk1/2) antibodies. The quantified signal intensities are presented as a ratio of the protein of choice to the corresponding GAPDH values normalized to the value of unstimulated LEC. Data are representative of three experiments (mean + SEM). *p<0.05, *p<0.01.
