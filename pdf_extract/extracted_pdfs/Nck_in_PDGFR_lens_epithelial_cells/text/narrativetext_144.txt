Generation of Nck1 knockdown LEC (shNck1 LEC). _ In order to investigate Nck1 protein functions in LEC, the pLVX-shRNA vector encoding an Nck1-specific short hairpin RNA (pLVX-shNck1) (Takara Shuzo, Tokyo, Japan) was constructed as previously described*’. The shRNA target sequence against Nck1 was as fol- lows: 5'-GGGTTCTCTGTCAGAGAAA-3”, The downregulation of Nck1 adaptor protein in LEC was achieved using pLVX-shNckl transfection. Transfection of the constructed vector into LEC was carried out with an elec- troporator using Amaxa Nucleofection II (Lonza). Cells were transfected with the Nckl-shRNA expressing vec- tors, pLVX-shNckl. A stable clone of Nck1 knockdown LEC was then obtained by culturing the transfected cells in media supplemented with puromycin followed by limiting dilution. The efficacy of Nck1 protein reduction and PDGER expression was assessed by western blotting analysis.
