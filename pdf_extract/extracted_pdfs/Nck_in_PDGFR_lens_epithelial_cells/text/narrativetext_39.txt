Figure 3. Nckl knockdown disturbs cell cycle progression of LEC. Flow cytometry was used to detect cell cycle distribution. After 24 h treatment with 30 ng/ml PDGF-BB, a propidium iodide (PI)-stained cells were analyzed for the percentage of cell population at each phase of the cell cycle. The percentages of cells in G1, S, and G2/M is shown in graph representing the mean + SEM. Data are representative of three independent experiments. ™*p <0.01, ***p<0.001. uns= unstimulated cells, PD = PDGF-BB-stimulated cells.
