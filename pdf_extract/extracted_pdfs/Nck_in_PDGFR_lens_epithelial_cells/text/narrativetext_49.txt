Figure 4. Knockdown of Nck1 inhibits PDGF-BB-induced LEC migration. A scratched wound was created in the absence or presence of 30 ng/ml PDGF-BB. The covered wound area quantification was acquired at 24 h. (A) Representative images of wound closure of LEC at 0 and 24 h. (B) Quantification of the wound area ratio is shown in graph representing the mean + SEM. Data are representative of three independent experiments. p< 0.01.
