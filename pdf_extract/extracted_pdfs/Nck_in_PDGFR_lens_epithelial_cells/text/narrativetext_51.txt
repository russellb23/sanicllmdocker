Knockdown of Nck1 inhibits PDGF-BB-induced LEC migration. Migration of LEC is a dynamic process involving multiple LEC cellular events”*. It has also been reported that LEC migration is a crucial patho- logical mechanism of cataract surgery-related complications, known as the PCO. The development of PCO is caused by the residual of LECs, in response to surgery, that migrate away from the anterior into the posterior lens capsule leading to recurrent vision loss**. Thus, inhibition of LEC migration might be useful for preventing PCO development. Since cell migration is sensitive to the proliferative state, the nonproliferating cells would not undergo migration. Moreover, our studies showed that signaling through Nck1 protein regulated both prolifera- tion and cell cycle arrest. We thus hypothesized that Nck1 knockdown might suppress migration of LEC. Fol- lowing this, the role of Nckl1 on LEC migration was assessed using a conventional scratch wound healing assay.
