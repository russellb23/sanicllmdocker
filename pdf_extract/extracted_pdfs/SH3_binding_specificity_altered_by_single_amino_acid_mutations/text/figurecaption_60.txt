FIG. 5. Analysis of SH3 binding sites in p68 with the wild-type and mutant SH3 domains. Schematic diagrams representing wild-type p68-Myc and its deletion constructs used in the experiment are illustrated on the left. The amino acid sequences of three known SH3 binding sites, P3, P4, and PS, are also shown. Each plasmid construct was transfected into HeLa cells, and 100 wl of each Triton cell lysate was incubated with glutathione-agarose bound with GST (lane 2), GST-Sre SH3 (lane 3), GST-Sre SH3 D99N (lane 4), GST-Abl SH3 (lane 5 and longer-exposure lane 8), GST-Abl SH3 F91Y/T98D (lane 6 and longer-exposure lane 9), or GST-Abl SH3 T98D (lane 7 and longer-exposure lane 10). The bound proteins were eluted with SDS sample buffer, fractionated on a 10% polyacrylamide gel along with 5 j1l of total cellular lysates (TCL) (lane 1), transferred to nitrocellulose, and probed with a mouse monoclonal antibody against Myc as described in Materials and Methods.
