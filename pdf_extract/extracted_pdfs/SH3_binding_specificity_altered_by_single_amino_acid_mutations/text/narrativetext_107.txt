Change of nomenclature. The Src SH3-associated p62 re- ported in our previous paper (24) has been renamed p68 in this paper since it has an apparent molecular mass of 68 kDa and does not constitute the major GTPase-activating protein-asso- ciated 62-kDa tyrosine-phosphorylated protein, as suggested by our unpublished results as well as the results from other groups (7, 12, 22, 25).
