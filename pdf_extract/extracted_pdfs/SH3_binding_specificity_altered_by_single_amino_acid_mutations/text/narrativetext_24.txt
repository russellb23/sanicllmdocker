In addition, the substitution of Asp in the analogous position Abl SH3 altered the binding properties of this protein and conferred binding activity to ligands contain- ing an Arg at the —3 position. These studies highlight the critical role the Asp-99 position in the binding selectivity of Src SH3 and provide the first example of protein engineering to shift the binding specificity of an SH3 domain.
