Affinity binding assay. The affinity binding assay was performed as described before (23, 24). Briefly, BALB/c 3T3 cells were labeled overnight with 50 Ci of [°S]methionine and then lysed with radioimmunoprecipitation assay (RIPA) buffer (158 mM NaCl, 5 mM EDTA, 10 mM Tris [pH 7.2], 0.1% sodium dodecyl sulfate [SDS], 1% sodium deoxycholate, 1% Triton X-100). Following clarifica- tion at 28,000 X g, supernatants were incubated for 4 h at 4°C with 50 yl of glutathione-agarose (1:1 slurry) bound with 50 yg of GST or GST-SH3 fusion proteins. After washing, the binding proteins were eluted with Laemmli sample buffer and fractionated on an SDS-8% polyacrylamide gel. The gel was soaked in 1 M sodium salicylate for 1 h, dried, and exposed to film at —70°C.
