We recently mapped the SH3 binding site in hnRNP K to a 36-amino-acid region containing two proline-rich stretches (RGPPPPP and RNLPLPP). Since both of these two proline-rich stretches contain an Arg_, to poten- tially interact with Src Asp-99, it is not surprising that binding of hnRNP K to Sre SH3 is dependent on Src Asp-99.
