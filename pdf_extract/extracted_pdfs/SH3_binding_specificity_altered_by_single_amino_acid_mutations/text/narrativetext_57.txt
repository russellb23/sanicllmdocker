HeLa cells expressing the wild-type epitope-tagged p68 (p68- Myc) or its deletion mutants were lysed with Triton buffer, and the lysates were incubated with GST-SH3 fusion proteins and glutathione-agarose. Bound proteins were transferred to nitro- cellulose and detected with antibody 9E10, which recognizes the Myc epitope tag. Consistent with the results shown in Fig. 4B, p68-Myc bound well to the wild-type Src SH3, and the D99N mutation in Src SH3 resulted in approximately 50% of p68 binding (Fig. 5A, lanes 3 and 4).
