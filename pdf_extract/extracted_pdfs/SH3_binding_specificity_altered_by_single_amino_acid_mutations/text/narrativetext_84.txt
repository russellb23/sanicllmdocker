In an attempt to understand the structural basis for these two different binding mechanisms, we compared the SH3 bind- ing sites in hnRNP K and p68. In hnRNP K, the SH3 binding site has been mapped to a 36-amino-acid region containing two proline-rich stretches (RGPPPPP and RNLPLPP) (24). The presence of an Arg_, in both motifs can explain the strong dependence on Asp-99 for hnRNP K binding. On the other hand, p68 contains three SH3 binding sites: P3 (PPPPPVPR GR), P4 (RGVPPPP), and P5 (PLPPPAP). Src SH3 bound with similar efficiencies to the P4 site (with an N-ter- minal Arg_, that is similar to a class I SH3 ligand) and P3 site (with two C-terminal arginines similar to class II ligands), yet showed better recognition of the PS site (which lacks an Arg). We thus hypothesized that binding of the arginine-con- taining sites (P3 and P4) to the Src SH3 domain would be dependent on Src Asp-99, whereas binding of P5, which lacks an arginine, would be independent of Src Asp-99. The results from the affinity binding assay using the p68 deletion con- structs containing either P3, P4, or P5 supported this hypoth- esis (Fig. 5).
