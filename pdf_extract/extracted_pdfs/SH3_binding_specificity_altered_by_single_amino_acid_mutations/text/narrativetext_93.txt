It is somewhat puzzling that the P5 binding site, which ex- hibited efficient SH3 binding, lacks an Arg, since Arg has been clearly shown to be important for high-affinity binding of SH3 peptide ligands to Src SH3 (for example, we found that the RPL peptide has an eightfold-higher affinity for the Src SH3 domain than the YPL peptide does [Table 1]). One possible explanation is suggested by recent findings from phage display indicating that the residues flanking the core Src SH3 binding motif RPLPPLP can contribute significantly to the affinity of a ligand for the SH3 domain (18). Hence, the flanking sequence interactions could compensate for the absence of the Arg-Asp interaction involved in the p68-P5 fragment binding to Src SH3.
