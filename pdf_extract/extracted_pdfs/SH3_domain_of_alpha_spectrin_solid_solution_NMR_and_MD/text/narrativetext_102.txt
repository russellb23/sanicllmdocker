Ensemble MD Simulations Based on eNOE Restraints MD simulations have previously been combined with experi- mental data in an integrative manner, where MD trajectories are biased by experimental restraints, such as, e.g., NOE data, chemical shifts, or order parameters s? (Lindorff-Larsen et al., 2005; Papaleo et al., 2018; Richter et al., 2007). For validation of data consistency within the set of methodology shown here, we carried out additional, restrained ensemble MD simulations that used eNOE-derived distance restraints as bias potentials (see the STAR Methods for details.) Our restrained ensemble simulation scheme consisted of carrying out parallel MD simula- tions of 20 replicas for 100 ns each under the influence of flat- bottom harmonic biasing potentials that penalize deviations of the ensemble average of the distances from the experimentally derived reference values (see the STAR Methods).
