Here, we have demonstrated the constructive interplay of complementary techniques for assessment of protein dynamics in asmall protein domain, focused on addressing the biologically important aspects of protein motion. The work, involving MD simulations, NMR relaxation/recoupling measurements, and spatial dynamics by eNOE-based multi-state structure determi- nation principally as stand-alone techniques, shows a robust overall agreement between all methods.
