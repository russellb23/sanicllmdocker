The weight of each piece of the experimental data used was applied as originally established on GB3 (Vdgeli et al., 2015a) and tested on a set of other systems (Végeli et al., 2013; Vdgeli et al., 2016; Vdgeli et al., 2015). eNOE multi-state structure calculations were run using 100 initial random-structures and 100,000 torsion angle annealing steps. The final 3-state structure calculation used 300 starting structures and 300 000 torsion angle annealing steps. PROCHECK and Molprobity evaluation results are shown in Ta- ble S1C.
