Backbone and all-atom conformational distributions were determined using RMSD between either bundle members (see main text for details), snapshots in MD calculations (see Method Details), or comparing outcomes of replica runs. Bundle angular properties shown in Table Si were evaluated using PROCHECK (Laskowski et al., 1993) and MolProbity (Chen et al., 2010). Validity of individual methods is assessed by external validation using complementary technical approaches (see main text). This study does not employ significance criteria for proofing or rejecting hypotheses made.
