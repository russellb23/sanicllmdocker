A different recent concept assessing conformational space is based on the accurate measurement of (conflicting) proton-pro- ton internuclear distances (exact NOEs or eNOEs) (Végeli, 2014; Vogeli et al., 2009, 2014) and relies mainly on the exponential na- ture of the relationship between magnetization transfer efficiency and intermolecular distance. This over-proportional relation leads to the shortening of distance restraints, and thus inconsis- tencies with other restraints, in the presence of conformational motions (Figure 1C). The difference between the internuclear dis- tances of the single average structure and the precise restraints can now be exploited to yield information about the conforma- tional space of the protein (Végeli et al., 2016).
