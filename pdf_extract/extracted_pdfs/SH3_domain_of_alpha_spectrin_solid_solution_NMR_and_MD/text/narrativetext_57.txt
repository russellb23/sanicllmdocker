Here, we are deliberately selecting the three non-integrative techniques, NMR relaxation, MD simulation, and multi-state eNOE structure calculation, to compare the outcome of the indi- vidual approaches without mutual bias. Subsequently, we strengthen our comparative assessment by comparison of the individual techniques with NMR-restrained ensemble MD simu- lations that use the eNOE-derived distance restraints as bias po- tentials. We show that, together with the timescale information from regular NMR relaxation, the multi-state approach experi- mentally and independently validates the details of MD simula- tions (and vice versa). Consequently, the parallel assessment of the SH3 domain of chicken «-spectrin via these four method- ological frameworks yields a consistent picture and detailed in- sights into protein motion, which underlines the high value of ex- isting and future integrative approaches.
