SH3 domains are ~60-80 amino acid protein-protein interaction domains that bind to proline-rich (PR) sequences, often containing the core PXXP motif® (Fig. 1A). The human proteome encodes more than 300 different SH3 domains. PR motifs are abundant in the human proteome and do not need post-translational modifications for binding. Most SH3 domains display moderate to low affinities towards PR motifs (with dis- sociation constants, K,s, of 1-100 uM) and cross-react with different PR motifs’.
