We next examined whether Fyn SH3 binding to p85 affects the interaction of phosphotyrosine peptides with the SH2 domains. For this, we used a peptide derived from the platelet-derived growth factor receptor (PDGER) residues 738-755 (herein referred to as pY). This pY peptide contained two phosphorylated tyrosines (Y740 and Y750), known to bind both the nSH2 and cSH2 domains of p85*°. The pY peptide bound tightly to p85ASH3 in a 1:1 ratio (Ky = 0.66 + 0.10 uM), but showed no interaction with Fyn SH3 [see entries in light blue in Fig. 3C; Supplementary Fig. 3]. When pY was co-present with p85ASH3 in the ITC cell, the affinity of Fyn SH3 for p85ASH3 decreased moderately (Kz shifted from 10.0 + 1.4 uM to 28.7 + 4.5 uM), accompanied by changes in enthalpy and entropy con- tributions. Conversely, the presence of Fyn SH3 only slightly reduced pY binding to p85ASH3, consistent with the ~15-fold higher affinity of pY for p85ASH3 than for Fyn SH3. pY bound both Chimera 1 and Chimera 2 with an affinity and thermodynamics equivalent to its interaction with p85ASH3. However, neither did the presence of pY change the association of Fyn SH3 with the chimeras, nor did the presence of the Fyn $H3 alter the association of the peptide with the chimeras [Fig. 3B; see entries in white in Fig. 3C]. These results suggested that pY and Fyn SH3 do not bind to overlapping regions on the SH2. The modest anticooperative effects observed in asso- ciations with p85ASH3 may reflect allosteric influences mediated by ele- ments absent in the chimeras.
