Adding 2M pY peptide at the same time as the SH3 domains increased PI3K activation by Fyn WT, but not by Fyn R96I or R96W (Fig. 5A). The PI3K activation by Fyn R96I or R96W alone was already on par or higher compared to the combined activation by Fyn WT and the 2 uM pY peptide. We hypothesised that, compared to Fyn WT, the Fyn R961 or R96W variants more effectively engaged with p85ASH3 regions that overlap with pY activating interactions, eliminating the effect of adding pY.
