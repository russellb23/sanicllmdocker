Fig. 4| NMR interaction mapping onto Fyn SH3 variants. A Heatmap displaying normalised chemical shift perturbation indices (CSPi) for SH3 domain residues upon PRI binding. The colour gradient from white to red represents increasing CSPi values (0-8). Grey indicates residues with minimal chemical shift changes. Black represents unassigned residues and prolines. B Chemical shift perturbation analysis of PRI titration with SH3 variants: Fyn SH3 WT (left), R96W (middle) and R961 (right). Orange bars indicate medium chemical shift changes, magenta bars show strong perturbations, red bars represent disappeared resonances, and black bars indicate unassigned residues. C Heatmap displaying peak appearance ratios during titration of labelled SH3 domains with unlabelled p85ASH3. The white-to-red gradient indicates increasing appearance ratios (normalised 0-10). Black represents unassigned residues and prolines. D Peak appearance ratio analysis for labelled SH3 variants titrated with unlabelled p85ASH3.
