Next, we investigated how the PR-distal L112 position of SH3 domains contributes to p85-mediated p1 10 activation. We generated the L112N and L112A mutations based on the WT Fyn SH3 domain. ITC titrations showed that these L112 mutant SH3 domains bound p85ASH3_ and p85PR1-BH-PR2? with the same affinity (Ky ~ 40 uM) and comparable AH and TAS contributions as the PR1 peptide, conversely to WT Fyn SH3 [Fig. 5D, see entries in white in Fig. 5E; Supplementary Fig. 3, Supple- mentary Table 1]. The slightly altered thermodynamics of the PRI inter- actions with WT or L112 mutant Fyn may reflect internal allostery”. Our differential scanning fluorimetry (DSF) measurements confirmed that the Fyn SH3 L112N and L112A variants had a melting temperature, Tm, of more than 65° C, and hence that altered p85 binding was not due to SH3 destabilisation in our assay (Supplementary Fig. 8). These observations corroborated that the PR-distal mutations directly affect Fyn SH3 associa- tions with p85ASH3.
