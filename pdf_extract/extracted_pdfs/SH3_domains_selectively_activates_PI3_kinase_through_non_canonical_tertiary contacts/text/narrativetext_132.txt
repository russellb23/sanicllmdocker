To confirm the functional effect of the SH3 mutations directly in cells, we measured the phosphorylation of AKT on serine 473 (S473). S473 phosphorylation is a downstream effect of PI3K activation™, and serves as a 3 le readout for PI3K activation. We transfected EFE-184 cells with Fyn SH. constructs (WT, R96W and L112N) that included the full N-terminal flexib! region to promote native-like membrane anchoring of the SH3 domain. IP with an anti-p85a antibody followed by western blotting revealed that the transfected SH3 constructs associated with p85 as expected, based on their relative in vitro affinities (Supplementary Fig. 7C, D). A separate western blot analysis showed that EFE-184 cells transfected with WT or R96W Fyn SH3 contained significantly more AKT phosphorylated at $473 than mock- transfected cells. Conversely, L112N-transfected cells did not show increased $473 phosphorylation (Fig. 5C, Supplementary Fig. 7E, F). We observed a trend of stronger AKT phosphorylation in R96W-expressing cells compared to WT-expressing cells, consistent with our in vitro assays. However, this trend was not statistically significant under the conditions used, and further validation in other cell models and experimental conditions is warranted.
