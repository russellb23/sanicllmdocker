Using ITC, we demonstrated that all SH3 domains could bind to p85ASH3 within a limited range of affinities (Kys of 7-24 uM), com- parable to those of Fyn R96I and R96W [Fig. 6B, see entries in yellow in Fig. 6C]. In our lipid kinase assays, Grb2 cSH3 and Hck $H3 activated PI3K similarly to Fyn SH3. Despite their similar affinity to p85ASH3, the SH3 domains of p85 and Ubs3a failed to activate PI3K (Fig. 6D). We concluded that the stereochemistry of the PR-distal tertiary sites, rather than the binding affinity, correlated with the capability of these SH3 domains to activate PI3K.
