To further elucidate the tertiary contacts between SH3 domains and PI3K, we used bissulfosuccinimidyl suberate (BS3) to crosslink amine groups between SH3 domains and p85 constructs. Crosslinked proteins were separated on SDS-PAGE gel, trypsinised and analysed by MS (Supple- mentary Fig. 9). We first applied XL-MS to Fyn SH3 in complex with p85 Chimera 1 and Chimera 2, as those were the minimal systems that indicated tertiary interactions. Fyn SH3 contains only two lysines (Lys105 and Lys108), located on the base of the RT loop, approximately 15 A from residues 96 and 112. Given the 114A crosslinking distance of BS3, these lysines are within range to capture tertiary interactions near positions 96 and 112. XL-MS identified crosslinks between these lysines and the cSH2 domain in Chimera 1. In Chimera 2, additional crosslinks were detected to C-terminal lysines of nSH2, which are absent in Chimera 1. These results corroborated that Fyn SH3 associates with both nSH2 and cSH2 domains of p85.
