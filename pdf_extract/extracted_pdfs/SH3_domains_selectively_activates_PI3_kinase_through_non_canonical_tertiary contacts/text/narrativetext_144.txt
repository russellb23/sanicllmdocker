Expanding from this result, we crosslinked SH3 domains from Fyn, Grb2 and p85 with p85ASH3 using BS3. Grb2 cSH3 and p85 SH3 each contain three lysines. XL-MS identified eight crosslinks for Fyn, twelve for Grb2 cSH3 and twenty-one for p85 SH3 (Fig. 7A; Supplementary Tables 2-5). Fyn SH3 and Grb2 cSH3 showed similar crosslinking patterns, primarily involving lysines in the nSH2, iSH2 and cSH2 domains of p85. Grb2 cSH3 also crosslinked to the N-terminal amide of p85ASH3, located seven residues upstream of PRI. In contrast, p85 SH3 exhibited broader interaction patterns, including crosslinks to the BH domain and more extensive contacts with iSH2 (12 crosslinks, compared to five and six for Fyn and Grb2, respectively).
