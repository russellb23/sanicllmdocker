Fig. 5 | Lipid kinase assays showing the effects of the Fyn R96 and L112 mutants on PI3K activation. An equal volume of immunoprecipitation eluent was allocated to each reaction to ensure consistent amounts of immunoprecipitated p110 protein. For each set of experi- ments, a reaction without added peptide or SH3 served as the control. PIP3 values of the control reactions were set to 1 and used to normalise the PIP3 values within each set of experiments. Immunoprecipitation using normal IgG instead of anti-p110a antibody in the control sample was performed in parallel to measure background signal. Data in graphs represent levels of PIP3 after subtracting the absorbance of IgG samples and are presented as the mean of triplicates + $.D.
