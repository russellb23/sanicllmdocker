As a result, specific PI3K activation relies on the matching non- canonical tertiary interactions between SH3 domains and the SH2 regions of p85, rather than affinity. Notably, the strongest PI3K activation was achieved by Fyn with the PR-proximal R96W mutation, rather than the WT Fyn, despite the latter having a higher affinity for p85ASH3. We propose that R96W activates PI3K more effectively than R96 because R96W stabilises the p85 SH2 domains more firmly in a non-inhibitory conformation. This increased stabilisation incurs a penalty in apparent binding energy and alters the thermodynamic parameters (AH and TAS) for this interaction. Despite the ‘R’ position being highly variable in SH3 domains, it is normally not a tryptophan in vertebrates. However, the SH3 domain of v-Sre, the trans- forming gene product of the avian Rous sarcoma virus, contains a trypto- phan in this position, although v-Src derives from chicken c-Src that encodes an arginine there. Cell transformation by v-Src enhances activation of the PI3K pathway’, raising the possibility that the tryptophan-containing v-Src SH3 domain contributes to this phenotype. Interestingly, point mutations of the FYN gene at R96 and L112 could be detected in multiple human cancer patient samples”. In particular, R96W was found in cancer of breast, colon or prostate, further raising the possibility of its role in cell transformation.
