The tertiary selection of SH3 activators by PI3K likely acts in concert with other regulatory mechanisms (such as colocalization, co-expression, post-translational modifications, or multivalent interactions) to ensure signal fidelity. In cells, SH3-mediated PI3K activation would also compete with other SH3-binding partners. For example, Src family kinases like Fyn and Hck are autoinhibited in their inactive state, with the SH3 domain engaged intramolecularly'. Their activation (e.g. through the B cell antigen receptor, receptor tyrosine kinases, integrins, or dephosphorylation of the C-terminal tyrosine*”***°) may be required to expose the SH3 domain for PI3K binding. The balance between autoinhibition and competing interactions likely plays a key role in regulating PI3K activation and war- rants further investigation.
