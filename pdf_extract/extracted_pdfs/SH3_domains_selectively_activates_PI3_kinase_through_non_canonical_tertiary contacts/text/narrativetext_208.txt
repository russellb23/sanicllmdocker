Membrane-associated loci where PI3K is activated, often within receptor-assembled signalosomes, are enriched in SH3-containing proteins. Although some examples of highly specific SH3:ligand interactions exist”, a significant cross-reactivity has been observed for SH3 domains of higher eukaryotes”. Our results infer that many of these nonspecific SH3 domains may bind to the PR1 region of p85, but only few SH3 domains will trigger a functional outcome using appropriate tertiary interactions. By spatially and mechanistically uncoupling ‘binding’ from ‘activation’, cells can control PI3K activation amidst promiscuous SH3 domains.
