The separation of binding and activation might also enable additional biological functions. For example, this mechanism would allow non- activating SH3 domains to recruit and possibly sequester PI3K to a sub- cellular environment without stimulating its enzymatic activity. The same mechanism would allow negative feedback loops in which an ensemble of non-activating SH3 domains out-compete activating SH3 domains. This concept where the ligand binding mode determines the functional outcome is akin to the concept of functional selectivity most studied in G protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs), where different ligands of the same receptor can promote distinct cellular outcomes”. Functional selectivity might thus also enhance the ability of a cell to control signalling through protein interaction domains that are considered promiscuous based on their binding affinity.
