The SH3 domain of Fyn, including its mutants R961, R96W, L112A and L112N, spanning residues 82-144 of the human Fyn protein, were cloned into different expression vectors, namely pET 22b (Fyn and R961), pGEX- 2T (R96W) and pJEx411c (Fyn L112A and L112N). Other SH3 domains, such as the C-terminal SH3 domain of Grb2 (residues 151-217 of human Grb2) and Hck (residues 78-138 of human Hck), were cloned into the pGEX-2T vector. Additionally, the full-length human p85 (residues 1-724), p85ASH3 (residues 80-724), p85 PR1-BH-PR2 (residues 80-333) and p85ASH3APRI (residues 110-428), SH3 domain of p85 (residues 1-79) and Ubs3A (residues 276-340 of human Ubs3a) were cloned into the pGEX-6P1 vector. The p85 chimeras were cloned into the pJEx411c vector.
