All proteins were expressed in E. coli BL21 (DE3) cells with either a C-terminal His tag (for Fyn R96, Fyn R96I, Fyn L112A, Fyn L112N, p85 chimeral and chimera2) or a GST tag (for Fyn R96W, Grb2, Hck, Ubs3A. and all p85 constructs). The cells were grown to an OD of 0.6 at 600 nm, induced with 0.2 mM isopropyl-B-D-thio-galactoside (IPTG) and cultured overnight at 18 °C. Following induction, cells were pelleted by centrifugation and resuspended in a binding buffer. For His-tagged proteins, the binding buffer was 50 mM Tris (pH 8.0), 350mM NaCl, 10 mM Imidazole and 2 mM 6-mercaptoethanol. For GST-tagged proteins, the binding buffer was 50 mM Tris (pH 8.0), 350 mM NaCl, 2 mM EDTA and 2 mM DTT.
