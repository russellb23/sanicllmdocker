His-tagged proteins were fur- ther eluted by applying a gradient of the buffer containing 50 mM Tris (pH 8.0), 350 mM NaCl, 500 mM Imidazole and 2 mM f-mercaptoethanol. The GST-tagged proteins were cleaved from the GST tag by overnight incuba- tion with PreScission protease (for PGEX-6P1) or thrombin (for pGEX-2T) on beads. The eluted (His-tagged or GST-cleaved) SH3 domains and p85 chimeras were further purified by size exclusion chromatography on a Superdex 75 column (Cytiva HiLoad 16/600 Superdex 75), using a buffer containing 20 mM HEPES, 150 mM NaCl, 2 mM TCEP and 2 mM EDTA. For p85 proteins, the size exclusion was performed on a Superdex 200 column (Cytiva HiLoad 16/600 Superdex 200). The eluted peak fractions were concentrated to 3-5 mg/ml and used for further experiments. The purity of all SH3 domain proteins was verified on the SDS-PAGE (Sup- plementary Fig. 11).
