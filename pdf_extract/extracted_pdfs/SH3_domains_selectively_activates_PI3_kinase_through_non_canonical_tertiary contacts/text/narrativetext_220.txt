Protein expression and purification. Fyn R96 and its mutants R96I and R96W were expressed in E. coli BI21 (DE3) cells. The cells were cultured in 1 L of 1X M9 medium that contains 1 g of "N ammonium chloride (Cambridge Isotope) and 2 g of °C D-glucose (Cambridge Isotope) at 37°C to an OD of 0.6 at 600 nM. Protein expression was induced with 0.4 mM IPTG, and the cells were then incubated overnight at 25 °C. The cells were then harvested and processed for protein purification. Fyn and R96I were purified using the His-tag protocol. R96W was purified fol- lowing the GST-tag protein purification method as described above.
