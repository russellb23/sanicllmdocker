NMR samples preparation and data recording. Protein samples were dialysed into 20 mM sodium phosphate (pH 7.4), 150 mM NaCl, 1 mM TECP and 0.02% Sodium Azide 90%/10% H30/D,0. All NMR experi- ments were performed on a Bruker 700 MHz Avance III spectrometer equipped with the TCI 700 H-C/ N-D CryoProbe. Unless stated other- wise, experiments were measured at the temperature carefully adjusted to 25°C. Assignment of backbone 'H, '°N and ‘°C resonances was done basing on standard set of NMR experiments: 2D 'H,°N HSQC, 'H,"C HSQC spectra” (tuned separately to aliphatic and aromatic carbons), HNCO"', HNCA”, HNCACB™, CACB(CO)NH™, HN(CA)CO”, per- formed on a sample containing 500uM “C,"N-labelled Fyn R96I. Assignment of Fyn WT and Fyn R96W proteins was obtained by com- parison of 'H,'°N HSQC spectra with assigned 'H,'""N HSQC obtained for Fyn R96I. Backbone connectivities were confirmed in case of both pro- teins by analysis of CACB(CO)NH and HNCACEB. To obtain reliable assignment in 5 °C we additionally measured 'H,’°"N HSQC and 'H,°C HSQC at 5 °C, 10 °C, 15 °C, 20 °C for Fyn R96I (with all the data collected for Fyn R96 and R96W, the measurement at 5 °C was sufficient to transfer assignment to lower temperature).
