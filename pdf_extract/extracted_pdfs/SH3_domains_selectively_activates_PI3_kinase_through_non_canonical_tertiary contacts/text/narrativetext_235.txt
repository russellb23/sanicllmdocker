Fig. 7 | Molecular mechanism for the activation of p85 by SH3 domains. A Mapping the intermolecular cross-links between p85ASH3 and SH3 domains from p85 and Fyn, as well as between Fyn SH3 and Chimera 2. Cross-links are shown as black lines between the approximate positions in the schematic protein repre- sentations. B Two 90° views showing XL-MS-mapped lysines on the p110:p85ASH3 heterodimer. p110 is shown as a surface model with the catalytic domain in light grey, and other domains in dark grey. Domains of p85ASH3 are colour-coded. Lysines identified in XL-MS and being compatible with a membrane-associated p110:p85 complex are shown as sphere models, coloured in blue (crosslinked only in presence of Fyn SH3), purple (only Grb2 cSH3), or red (crosslinked by both Fyn and Grb2 SH3).
