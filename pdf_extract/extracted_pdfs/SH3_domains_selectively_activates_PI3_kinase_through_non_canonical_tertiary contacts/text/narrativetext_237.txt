2D 'H-"®N HSQC experimental parameters. All spectra were recorded on a 700 MHz AVANACE NEO NMR spectrometer equipped with a Bruker TXO ('H/"C/N/’H) cryogenic probe (BrukerBioSpin, Rhein- stetten, Germany) at 278 K. The spectra were processed and corrected for phase and baseline distortions using Topspin 4.1.4 (Bruker BioSpin). 2D 'H-PN HSQC NMR spectra were acquired using hsqcfpf3gpphwg experiments. The number of scans varied according to peak intensities or protein concentrations. A recycle delay of 1 s and a per-scan acquisition time of 90 ms were used. The spectral width was set to 1915.923 Hz in the F1 dimension and 11363.637 Hz in the F2 dimension, with data points of 256 and 2048, respectively. A QSINE window function, 0.3 Hz line broadening, and SSB set to zero were applied in both dimensions.
