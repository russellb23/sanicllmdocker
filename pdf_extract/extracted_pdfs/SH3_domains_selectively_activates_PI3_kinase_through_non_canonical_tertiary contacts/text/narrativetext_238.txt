All 2D NMR experiments were processed using NMRpipe”, all 3D NMR experiments were measured with sparse sampling of indirectly detected dimensions to increase the resolution and were processed using qMDD*” and analysed using SPARKY software (Goddard, T.D. an Kneller, D.G., SPARKY3: University of California, San Francisco). Chemical shift changes for CSP analysis of 'H and '°N nuclei measured in ppm (AH and AN) were calculated using the CSP (ppm) formula”. The median an: IQR values were calculated (equation IQR = 1.5 x (Q; — Qi, where Q; is the first quartile, and Q; is the third quartile). The line cutoff value was obtained by the addition of the median to IQR”. To account for the lower dispersion of '°N chemical shifts relative to 'H, the '°N shift differences were scaled by a factor of 0.2, as recommended to balance their contribution in the combined chemical shift perturbation (CSP) calculation”. The total CSP values were then computed according to the standard formula, and median values were determined. Data visualisation was performed using Prism 10.0 GraphPa Software, Boston, Massachusetts, USA. www.graphpad.com.
