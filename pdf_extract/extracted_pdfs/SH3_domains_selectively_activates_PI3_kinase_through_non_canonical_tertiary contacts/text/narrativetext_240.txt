ITC experiments were conducted using a MicroCal PEAQ-ITC (Malvern Panalytical) and a MicroCal iTC200 (GE Healthcare), following the stan- dard 19-injection method at 25°C. All proteins were dialysed in 20 mM sodium phosphate (pH 7.5) with 150 mM NaCl, 2mM EDTA and 1-2 mM TCEP, then degassed before titration. For peptide-to-protein titrations, lyophilised peptides (p85 PR1, p85 PR2 and PDGFR pY) were dissolved in the dialysate and loaded into the syringe. When titrating p85 domains, the SH3 domains were placed in the syringe. Protein concentrations in the cell ranged from 20 to 75 uM, with at least a 10-fold higher concentration in the syringe. Data were analysed using Origin and PEAK ITC software.
