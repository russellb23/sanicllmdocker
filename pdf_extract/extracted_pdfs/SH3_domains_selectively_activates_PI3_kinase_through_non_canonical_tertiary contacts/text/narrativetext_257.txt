Peak lists in the format of megf files were submitted for searching through the Protein Prospector ‘Batch-Tag Web’ tool”. Each spectrum’s peaks were queried with a precursor charge range of 2-5, a tolerance of 20 ppm for precursor ions, and 1 Da for fragment ions. The instrument setting defined was ESI-Q-hi-res. Cleavage selectivity was adjusted to trypsin, allowing for up to two missed cleavages per peptide. Carbamidomethy1 [C] and oxidation [M] were designated as constant modifications. The Protein Prospector ‘Search Compare’ programme was used to generate a report on Crosslinked Peptides.
