cDNAs encoding the wild type Fyn (1-146), or its mutants R96W, L112N, each with the native N-terminal flexible region and a C-terminal HA tag, were cloned into lentiviral vector pT wist Lenti SFFV Puro WPRE (Twist Bioscience, San Francisco, CA). To produce lentivirus, HEK293-FT cells were seeded at a density of 1 x 10° in 6-well plates. The cells were transfected with 2 ug of the expression plasmid along with lentivirus packaging plas- mids (1.5 ug psPAX2 and 0.5 ug pMD2.G) using Lipofectamine 3000
