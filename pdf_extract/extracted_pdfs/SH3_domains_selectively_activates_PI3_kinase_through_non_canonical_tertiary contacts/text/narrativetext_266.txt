Protein was harvested using RIPA buffer, which consisted of 1% sodium deoxycholate, 1% NP-40, 150 mM NaCland 0.1% sodium dodecyl-sulphate (SDS) supplemented with protease and phosphatase inhibitors (Thermo Fisher Scientific). The protein samples were mixed with 6x SDS loading buffer (25% glycerol, 62.5 mM Tris-HCl (pH 6.8), 2% SDS, 0.6 M DTT and 0.01% bromophenol blue), before being subjected to SDS-polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis and transferred onto a polyvinylidene fluoride (PVDF) membrane (Millipore, Billerica, MA). The membrane was then blocked with 5% non-fat milk in Tris-buffered saline with Tween 20 (150 mM NaCl, 20 mM Tris, pH 7.5, 0.1% Tween 20) at room temperature, followed by incubation with a primary antibody overnight at 4°C and a horseradish peroxidase-conjugated secondary antibody for an additional 2h at room temperature. Protein detection was carried out using chemiluminescence kit (Bio-Rad, Hercules, CA). Densitometry analysis of the western blots was conducted using Image J. The primary antibodies used are: anti-p110a antibody (Cell Signaling Technology (CST), Beverly, MA; #4249; 1:1000), anti-p110B antibody (CST#3011; 1:1000), anti-p1105 antibody (CST#34050; 1:1000), anti-p110y antibody (CST#5405; 1:1000), anti-p85a antibody (Santa Cruz sc-71892; 1:200), anti-p85B (Abcam, Cambridge, United Kingdom; ab28356; 1:1000), anti-HA antibody (Biolegend, San Diego, CA; #901501; 1:2000), antic AKT pS473 (CST#9271; 1:1000), anti- AKT antibody (CST#4691; = 1:3000), ~—anti-tubulin —_ antibody (CST#2148; 1:4000).
