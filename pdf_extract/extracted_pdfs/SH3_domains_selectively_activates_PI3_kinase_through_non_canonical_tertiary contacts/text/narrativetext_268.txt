For mass spectrometry (MS), 10 mg protein lysates were used for IP with anti-p110a antibody or IgG control at a 1:50 dilution. The eluted proteins were separated on an 8% SDS-PAGE gel and visualised using either silver staining or liquid chromatography tandem MS (LC-MS/MS) analysis conducted at the Centre for PanorOmic Sciences at the University of Hong Kong. To prepare the samples for LC-MS/MS, gel sections were reduced using 10mM TCEP (Thermo Fisher Scientific), alkylated with 55 mM 2-chloroacetamide (Sigma-Aldrich, St. Louis, MO), and digested with trypsin at a concentration of 1 ng/uL overnight at 37°C. Subsequently, tryptic peptides were extracted from the gel in a sequential manner using 50% Acetonitrile (ACN; J.T. Baker) and 5% formic acid (Thermo Fisher Scientific), followed by 100% ACN, and desalted using C18 StageTips. The eluted peptides were then analysed using nanoelute UHPLC coupled to a Bruker timsTOF pro mass spectrometer. The raw mass spectrometry data underwent processing using MaxQuant 1.6.14.0 and were searched against the Human UniProt FASTA database.
