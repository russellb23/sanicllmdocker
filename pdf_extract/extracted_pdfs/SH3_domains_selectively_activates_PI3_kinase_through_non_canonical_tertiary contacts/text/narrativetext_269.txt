To detect the protein interaction between p85a and Fyn SH3 domains, in vivo crosslinking was performed using 0.5 mM dithiobis(succinimidyl propionate)(DSP; Sigma-Aldrich) prior to IP. Briefly, cells in culture dishes were washed with cold PBS before incubation with freshly prepared DSP solution for 30min at 37°C. The reaction was stopped by adding a quenching buffer (Tris-HCl 20 mM, pH 7.4). After 15 min, the culture dish was rinsed with cold PBS twice before cell lysis with IP buffer. Anti-p85a antibody (Santa Cruz sc-71892) at 1:50 was used for IP.
