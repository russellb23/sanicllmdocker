We chose the Fyn SH3 domain to investigate its activation of PI3K. Renzoni and colleagues previously studied the binding of Fyn SH3 to p85a (for simplicity, we hereafter refer to p85a and p110a as p85 and p110, respec- tively). They observed that the almost complete p85 containing PR1 (p85ASH3; the N-terminal p85 SH3 domain was deleted to avoid effects from intramolecular competition) binds to the Fyn SH3 domain with a higher affinity and different thermodynamic parameters compared to the isolated PR1 peptide”. It was hypothesised that the differences resulted from p85 interactions outside the canonical class I PXXP motif (R°PLPVAP).
