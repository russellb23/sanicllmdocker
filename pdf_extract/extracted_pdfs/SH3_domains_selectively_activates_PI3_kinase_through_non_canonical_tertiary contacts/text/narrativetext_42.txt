Our isothermal titration calorimetry (ITC) experiments with Fyn SH3 and either a PRI peptide or p85ASH3 confirmed that Fyn SH3 bound p85ASH3 with a fourfold higher affinity than the PR1 peptide (dissociation constant, K,, of 10.0 + 1.4 uM vs. 41.3 + 2.7 uM, respectively) [Fig. 2A; see entries in light grey in Fig. 2B]. This small but significant difference in affinity was further confirmed by microscale thermophoresis and fluores- cence anisotropy (Supplementary Fig. 1A). Moreover, the thermodynamic ITC profile for the reactions of Fyn SH3 with PR1 or p85ASH3 differed, showing that the PRI association was driven by enthalpy, whereas the p85ASH3 association was dominated by favourable entropy contributions (Fig. 2B). Together, these data supported that p85ASH3 engages Fyn SH3 in a mechanistically different interaction than PR1.
