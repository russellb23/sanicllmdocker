We used ITC to test if the SH3 ‘R’ position also affects SH3:p85 interactions. The Hck SH3 domain and the Fyn SH3 variants R96I and R96W all bound the PR1 peptide with comparable affinities (Kz ~ 40 uM) to Fyn WT, and all showed similar enthalpy-dominated binding profiles [Fig. 2A; see entries in light grey in Fig. 2B]. In contrast, interactions between these SH3 domains and p85ASH3 exhibited small but significant differences in both affinity and thermodynamic signatures—relative to their respective PRI interactions and to each other (Fig. 2), suggesting that the RT loop residue at position 96 modulates SH3 binding to p85ASH3, but not to PR1. A p85ASH3 construct lacking the PR1 region (p85ASH3APR1), did not bind to the SH3 domains, confirming that the association with p85ASH3 is driven by canonical interactions with PR1, rather than with PR2 [Fig. 2A; see entries in dark grey in Fig. 2B], in agreement with previous observations”.
