Supplementary Figure 2. PR1-BH-PR2 SAXS data. Ensemble Optimization Method (EOM) analysis SEC-SAXS data collected on the p85 PR1-BH-PR2 fragment. The BH domain (residues 121-299) was kept fixed, and the flanking PR1 and PR2 regions were allowed to be flexible. The radius of gyration (Rg; A) and maximum distance (Dmax; B) are shown in A for the structural pool (black) and the EOM selected representative structures (red). C) Fit of selected ensemble models (red line) to SAXS data (black dots) with chi? of 7.42. D) EOM-selected structures are shown superimposed on their BH domain (green). Collectively, the distributions of the selected molecules from the Rg and Dmax pools, and the conformations of the PR1 and PR2 regions in the structural models support that both PR regions are highly flexible and do not stably associate with the BH domain.
