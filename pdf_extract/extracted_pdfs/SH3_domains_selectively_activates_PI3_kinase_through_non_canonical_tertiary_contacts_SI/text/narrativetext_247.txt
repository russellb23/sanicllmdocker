Supplementary Figure 6. NMR analysis of p85 PR1 and Fyn. *H-°N HSQC spectra acquired during the Fyn titrations. Varying amounts of labelled °C,1°N-labelled Fyn R96, Fyn R96I, and Fyn R96W were added to 90 UM unlabelled PR1 samples. Each panel represents an overlay between unbound Fyn SH3 variants (black) and PR1-bound Fyn and its variants at different PR1:Fyn ratios. The colour scheme is 0:0.1, black; 1:0.5, red; 1:1, blue; 1:6, green.
