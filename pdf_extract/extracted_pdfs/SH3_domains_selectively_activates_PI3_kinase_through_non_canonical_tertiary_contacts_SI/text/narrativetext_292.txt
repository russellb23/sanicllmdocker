Supplementary Figure 8. Differential scanning fluorimetry (DSF) to determine the thermal stability of SH3 domains. A-C) The first negative derivative of the fluorescence intensity is plotted against the temperature for SH3 domains. A) Plots obtained from 50 uM of Fyn wild type (Fyn; red) and its mutants L112A (purple), L112N (black), R96W (blue), R9IG6I (green). B) As (A), but with a Y-axis scale adapted for the fluorescence intensity of R96I and R96W. The R9I6W mutant was additionally measured at 100 uM (dark blue). C) 50 uM of Hck (brown), p85 (dark olive), and Grb2 (teal). D) Tm values derived for each SH3 domain, presented as the mean of triplicates + S.D.
