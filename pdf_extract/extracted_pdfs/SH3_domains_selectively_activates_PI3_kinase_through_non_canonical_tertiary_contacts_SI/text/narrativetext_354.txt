Supplementary Data 1 (excel file of IP-MS). Validation of 110a pulldown after immunoprecipitation (IP) by mass spectrometry analysis (IP-MS). IP-MS was performed in triplicate, including both IgG control and specific anti-110a antibody pulldowns for each replicate. After filtering for proteins detected exclusively in p110a antibody pulldown and not in the IgG control, 110 proteins were consistently detected across all three triplicates. Among these, p110a was the most abundant, indicating efficient immunoprecipitation. No other p110 isoforms were detected. Consistent with our western blot results, both p85a and p85 were also identified.
