Supplementary Figure 1. A) Additional affinity measurements between Fyn SH3 and either the PR1 peptide or p85 ASH3, performed using microscale thermophoresis (MST) and fluorescence anisotropy. Individual symbols represent all experimental replicate measurements (n = 3) at each concentration, and solid lines indicate the fitted binding isotherms. B) Multiple sequence alignment of the synthetically designed chimera constructs. Domains are shaded according to the corresponding p85 regions: gray, PR1; gray, conserved linker; blue, nSH2; magenta, iSH2; yellow, cSH2. The sequences were aligned using Clustal Omega.
