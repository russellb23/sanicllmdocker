Figure 1. Calculated root-mean-square distance (rmsd) of backbone atoms of the SH3 domain of a-spectrin from MD simulations at temperatures of (a) 298 K, (b) 440 K, and (c) 500 K with respect to its X-ray crystal structure as a function of the simulation time. The black and red lines represent averages of rmsd values obtained from multiple independent simulation runs (4 at 298 and 500 K and 5 at 440 K) calculated by the GBMV2 parameters in the original implementation (GBMV2-/s20 parameter set, Bs = —20 and So = 0.7) and with a recently reformulated set of parameters (GBMV2-fs10 parameter set, 's = —10 and Sy = 0.57), respectively. Uncertainty is shown as error bars and was estimated by the standard deviation.
