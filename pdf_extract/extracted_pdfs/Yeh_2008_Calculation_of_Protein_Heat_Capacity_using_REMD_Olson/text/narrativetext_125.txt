Computational studies aimed at understanding the protein- folding process involve investigation beyond whether the simulations can sample the native and unfolded conformations correctly but must address issues of folding thermodynamics, for example, changes in the heat capacity on protein folding or unfolding and the corresponding melting curve of the protein. There are few reported studies on computing a protein heat capacity using all-atom MD simulations with implicit solvent models.
