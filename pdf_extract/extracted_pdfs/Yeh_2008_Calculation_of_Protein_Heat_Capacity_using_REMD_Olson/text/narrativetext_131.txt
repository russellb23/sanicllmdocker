For the CHARMM22 force field, we investigated the GBSW and GBMV2 solvent models and their parametrizations. The two surface-boundary parametrizations of GBSW were found to yield strikingly different results. Even though there is a small difference in the smoothing length between the two GBSW models (w = 0.3 A for GBSW1 and w = 0.2 A for GBSW2), the main difference is scaling of the CFA and its higher-order correction term.!°!327 The GBSW1 model was parametrized from fitting Born radii to a van der Waals-based surface with a smooth dielectric boundary and gives less weight to the CFA and more to the correction term than the GBSW2 parametriza- tion, which attempts to mimic a sharp molecular-surface boundary.
