Note that, unlike the standard protocol of computing ensemble averages of structural properties and their naturally occurring fluctuations over the entire equilibrated simulation trajectory, the unfolding—folding transition temper- atures were slowly decaying over time and configurational averaging of the density of states was restricted to the last 2500 conformations (0.5 ns) per temperature replica of the 10 ns simulation.
