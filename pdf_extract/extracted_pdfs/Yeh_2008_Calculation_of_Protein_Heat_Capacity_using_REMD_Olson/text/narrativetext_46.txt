We performed REMD simulations by running the program CHARMM with the MMTSB (Multiscale Modeling Tools for Structural Biology) Tool Set.28 The CHARMM22 force field was used to represent the protein in the GBMV2 and GBSW models, while the CHARMM19 force field was used for the GBORN model. We also ran a simulation with a distance- dependent protein dielectric model (¢ = 4r) to calculate the thermodynamics of protein folding.
