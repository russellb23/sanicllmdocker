In the work of ChocholouSova and Feig,”’ they reported that modifying the smoothing parameter fs of the GBMV2 model can enhance energy conservation in MD simulations when using this implicit solvent model. As a preliminary to applying replica- exchange simulations with the GBMV2 model, we tested the effect of modifying fs on simulation trajectories computed at several different constant temperatures. Figure 1 shows the calculated rmsd between backbone heavy atoms of the X-ray crystal structure of the SH3 domain and structures obtained from single-temperature MD simulations performed at temperatures of 298, 440, and 500 K with the two sets of GBMV2 parameters Bs = —10 and —20. The results show that the SH3 domain remained folded at 298 K and it became unfolded quickly at 500 K with both sets of parameters.
