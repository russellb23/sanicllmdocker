The contour map from the GBSW2 model with y = 0.03 kcal/mol: A2 shows that, near T., the folded state is distributed between two major clusters of SH3 conformations. A near-native cluster is observed at p ~ 0.8; however, it is less densely populated than the second cluster positioned at p ~ 0.5. The more populated cluster is higher in energy and is presumably favored by entropic contributions. For the GBSW2 model with y = 0.005 kcal/mol: A2, there is less bifurcation of the folded state, yet the ensemble of unfolded states now exhibits two disconnected clusters, one displaying significant population with native contacts at p ~ 0.15.
