NMR Spectroscopy, Structure Calculations, and Analysis. All NMR experiments were carried out at 25 °C on a Varian INOVA 800- or 600-MHz spectrometer, equipped with a '°N/'3C/'H triple-resonance cold probe and a z-axis gradient system. NMR spectra for structure determination of IRTKS SH3:EspFy R475 complex were recorded on two samples: 0.48-mM uniformly '°N, '?C-labeled IRTKS SH3 in 1:1 complex with unlabeled EspFy R475 and 0.90-mM uniformly "5x, C-labeled EspFy R475 in 1:1 complex with unlabeled IRTKS SH3.
