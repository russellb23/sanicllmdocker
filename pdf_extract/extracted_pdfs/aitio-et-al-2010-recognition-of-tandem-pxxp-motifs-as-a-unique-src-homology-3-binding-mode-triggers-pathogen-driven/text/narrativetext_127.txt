Peptide Array. Peptides of interest were synthesized by the Peptide and Protein Laboratory (Haartman Institute, University of Helsinki) as peptide- cellulose conjugates using Multipep (INTAVIS Bioanalytical Instruments) according to the manufacturer's protocol for SPOT synthesis (36) and printed in parallel arrays on glass slides using SlideSpotter equipment (INTAVIS Bioanalytical Instruments). Experimental details are provided in S/ Materials and Methods.
