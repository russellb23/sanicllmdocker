Construction of Plasmids Expressing EspFy Derivatives. The C-terminal proline- rich repeats of EspFy were deleted from plasmid pKC471 (13) by inverse PCR using primers flanked by Kpn! and BamHI restriction sites, creating plasmid pDV48. Plasmid pDV48 is designed to receive DNA inserts between sequences encoding the N-terminal translocation sequence of EspFy and a C- terminal 5-myc tag, and thus allows for expression of proteins that can be translocated into mammalian cells by the EHEC/enteropathogenic Escher- ichia coli (EPEC) type Ill secretion system. EspFy fifth repeat (R475, also re- ferred to as HP) was amplified by PCR and cloned into pDV48 to create plasmid pCL1. EspFy PHP WT and mutant fragments were synthesized (In- tegrated DNA Technologies) and cloned into pDV48, creating plasmids pCL2 (PHP), pDV168 (PHPP283"4), and pDV169 (PHP?39). All constructs were transformed into EPEC strain KC12, and their expression level was analyzed by Western blot using an anti-myc 9E10 monoclonal antibody (Santa Cruz Biotechnology).
