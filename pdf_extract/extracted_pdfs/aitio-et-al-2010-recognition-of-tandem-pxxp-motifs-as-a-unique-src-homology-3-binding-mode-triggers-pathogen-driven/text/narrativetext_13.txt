EspFy contains multiple 47-residue repeats (Fig. S1). The N- terminal 33-residue region (Fig. 1, “H”) of the EspFy repeats can activate the Wiskott-Aldrich syndrome protein (WASP) by contacting with its GTPase-binding domain to disrupt it from an intramolecular autoinhibitory interaction (15, 16). Our recent studies showed that the proline-rich C terminus of these repeats (Fig. 1, “P”) mediates recruitment of EspFy to sites of bacterial attachment by binding to the SH3 domain of IRTKS, which acts as an adapter connecting EspFy to Tir (10). We observed robust binding between the IRTKS SH3 and the complete 47-residue repeat of EspFy (R47s) but no binding to a 33-residue fragment (R335) lacking the C-terminal proline residues (10) (Fig. 1). Likewise, Stradal and colleagues (9) reported that IRSp53 SH3 can recognize the sequence IPPAPNWPAP in the C-terminal portion of the EspFy repeat.
