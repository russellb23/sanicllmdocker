In this study, we have used NMR spectroscopy as well as peptide array and mutagenesis approaches to characterize the molecular basis of the interaction between IRTKS SH3 and EspFy R475. Our IRTKS SH3:EspFy R47; complex structure reveals a unique type of SH3 interaction that involves accom- modation of two adjacent PPII helical PxxP motifs as the structural basis of IRTKS-EspFy binding, explaining the high affinity and selectivity of this interaction.
