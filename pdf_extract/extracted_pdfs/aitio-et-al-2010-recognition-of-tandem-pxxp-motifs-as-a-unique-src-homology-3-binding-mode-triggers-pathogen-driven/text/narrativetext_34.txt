We used both chemical shift perturbation (CSP) mapping and isothermal titration calorimetry (ITC) to determine the binding affinity of R47;. The CSP mapping showed that IRTKS SH3 associates with R47; with an affinity that is unusually high for SH3 binding, because the induced '°N/'H chemical shift changes observed in '°N-labeled IRTKS SH3 were in the regime of slow exchange in the NMR time scale (Fig. S2). This observation was confirmed more quantitatively using ITC, which indicated a dis- sociation constant (Ky) of 500 nM, ranking the IRTKS SH3: EspFy R47; interaction among the strongest found in naturally occurring ligands (Fig. S3).
