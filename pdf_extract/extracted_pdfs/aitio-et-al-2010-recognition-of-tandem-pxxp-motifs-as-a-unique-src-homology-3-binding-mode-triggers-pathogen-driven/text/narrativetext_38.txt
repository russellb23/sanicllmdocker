Structure of the IRTKS SH3:EspFy R47, Complex. The structure of the IRTKS SH3 in complex with EspFy R47; has a typical SH3 domain fold (Fig. 14). Five B-strands (61 to 65) and a short 319 helix establish the usual f-barrel fold, where the {2 strand is shared by two antiparallel B sheets. Strands 61 to B4 are con- nected by RT, n-Sre, and distal loops, whereas the 30 helix connects {4 to B5. The structure of the mouse ortholog of IRTKS SH3 has been solved by NMR in free form (PDB ID code 1spk). Superimposition of secondary structure elements of Ispk and IRTKS SH3 domain gives a Ca rmsd of 0.68 A, suggesting that the SH3 does not undergo any substantial structural rearrange- ments on binding to R47s.
