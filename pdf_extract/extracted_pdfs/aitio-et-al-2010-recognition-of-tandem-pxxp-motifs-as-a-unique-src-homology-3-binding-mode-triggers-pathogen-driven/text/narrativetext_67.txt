Both PxxP Motifs Are Required for EspFy Recruitment and Pedestal Formation by EHEC. EspFy alleles vary in the number of C- terminal 47-residue repeats, but all contain at least two repeats (25). As noted above, each EspFy repeat harbors two functional elements, a helical portion that binds and activates WASP/N- WASP (15, 16) and a proline-rich region that binds to IRTKS SH3 (9, 10) (Fig. 1C). Although a single proline-rich sequence is capable of recognition by the SH3 domain, two proline-rich sequences flanking a helical region, which we term PHP", are required for recruitment to sites of bacterial attachment and actin pedestal formation in mammalian cells. To investigate the relationship between SH3 recognition and EspFy recruitment to sites of bacterial attachment, myc-tagged PHP" was expressed in KC12, an E. coli derivative that expresses EHEC Tir and is capable of type III translocation. When fibroblast-like cells (FLCs) were infected with this strain, PHP’' was readily recruited to sites of bacterial attachment and promoted robust pedestal formation (Fig. 4, second row). In contrast, a variant of EspFy consisting of a single repeat (HP) was neither recruited nor mediated in pedestal formation (Fig. 4, first row).
