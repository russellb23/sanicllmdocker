Specific peptide recognition by IRTKS SH3 relies on the presence and correct positioning of the two consecutive PxxP motifs. Removal of an XP moiety from either the N or C ter- minus drastically impairs binding, indicating that the whole 13-aa epitope is indispensable (Fig. 3B). The molecular basis of proline recognition is analogous at both PPII helix docking sites. In a left-handed PPII helix conformation, the bond between N and C6 of the critical PxxP prolines points toward the hydrophobic surface of the SH3 domain. Any other natural amino acid would have a backbone NH proton at this position, resulting in re- pulsive interaction, and N-substitution of proline is thus required to prevent this unfavorable interaction (26). The high proline content of the tandem PxxP peptide is also likely to contribute to the affinity of binding through constrained conformational flex- ibility, resulting in reduced entropic cost on binding.
