Figure 2. Figure illustrating the “leaking” effect of using too small a void probe size in AVP. Images are of the protein structure 1js4A1. Spheres represent void points and clusters of void points of the same colour represent the same void. (a) Shows voids calculated using a void probe of radius 0.0 A. The vast majority of void points are dark blue, showing a huge void leaking though much of the protein interior. (b) Shows voids calculated with a void probe of radius 0.5 A. The space once occupied by the one huge void is now seen to be split into a number of different, large voids.
