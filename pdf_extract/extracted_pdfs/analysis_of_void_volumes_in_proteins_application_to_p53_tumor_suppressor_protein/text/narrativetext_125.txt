Of the 20 distinct cavity-creating mutations identified in p53 as likely to affect protein stability during this analysis, nine had already been explained by other factors in the automated method of Martin et al.?5 This left 11 mutations for which no explanation had previously been given. If these 11 distinct mutations are now included with those from the automated analysis, it is possible to explain 529 distinct (56.2%) or 10,453 observed (79.7%) mutations.
