In summary, we have developed a new method for analysis of void volumes in proteins. This has been applied to a non-redundant set of protein domains from CATH and used to assess void volumes in the p53 tumour suppressor protein. We have shown that packing quality is not, in general, correlated with protein stability and that poor packing does not seem to be a contributory factor to the low thermal stability of p53. However, mutations can open voids which may destabilize the native p53 fold sufficiently that it is no longer correctly folded at body temperature.
