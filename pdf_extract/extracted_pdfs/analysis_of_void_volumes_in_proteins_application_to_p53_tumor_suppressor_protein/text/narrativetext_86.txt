The total void volume of native p53, as calculated by AVP, is 2728.1 A®, and its largest void volume 157.5 A®. Both of these are within the normal range when compared with the volumes from the structures in our data set (see Figure 4). As voids of >275 A’ are seen in <20% of proteins and the largest void volume of each of the mutants in Table 3 is at least 1.8 X the size of the largest void in native p53, these are likely to have a detrimental effect on stability of the native p53 conformation.
