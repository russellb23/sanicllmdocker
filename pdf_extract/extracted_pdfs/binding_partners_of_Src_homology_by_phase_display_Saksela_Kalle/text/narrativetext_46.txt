We then generated an SH3 library specifically lacking a-PIX and B-PIX. A total of 29 clones from two independent experiments with the PIX-negative library were sequenced. Half of these (15 out of 29) contained Ncke-II/III. Ponsin-llI/III was found five times, vinexin-lIV/IIl three times and ArgBP2-llI/III twice. Notably, these three proteins are highly related, and SH3-III/III of the vinexin- ponsin-ArgBP2 family thus accounted for a third (10 out of 29) of these clones. The remaining SH3 domains were Src, Hck, Grb2-I/II and POSH2-III/IV (one of each).
