In case of stronger interactions (Kp <0.5 4M), the data points from serial SH3 dilutions could be nicely fitted on linear lines in Scatchard plots, and the apparent Kp values derived from these slopes (Fig 2B). This fit, together with a good correlation between the published values and the Kp values measured for PAK/B-PIX (24nM; Manser et al, 1998) and Nef/Hck (Lee et al, 1995) complexes here, indicated that the assay was valid and useful for estimation of binding affinities for these interactions. Despite their dose-dependent binding signals, modelling of weaker interactions by Scatchard plots was more problematic. Therefore, these interactions were assigned to two broad categories corresponding to estimated affinities in the range of 0.5-3 and 3-20yM. Interactions that were weaker than binding of Nef to Fyn-SH3 (Kp 15.8uM; Arold et al, 1998) or too weak to be detected at an SH3 concentration of 384M were assigned to a remaining category defined by a Kp value > 20 uM (Fig 3).
