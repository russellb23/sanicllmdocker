Our specificity map revealed that 35 of the 115 SH3 domains exhibit diverse non-canonical specificities and some domains exhibit multiple specificities. For convenience, we have arbi- trarily grouped these specificities into a single class IX, but these may be further subdivided into numerous subgroups with further characterization (Figure 3G). Structures of several of these do- mains in complex with peptides show the molecular details behind the unusual specificities.
