This process yielded 73,297 unique peptide sequences that were used to generate multiple binding specificity logos with the MUSI software, as described (Kim et al., 2012). This pipeline performs an internal-gap-free multiple sequence alignment of the peptide se- quences followed by an optimized clustering strategy to identify one or more specificities per domain. The motifs were described by a Position Weight Matrix (PWM) binding profile statistical model, which captured the frequency of amino acid preferences at each ligand position. Sequence logos were generated from the PWMs as a graphical representation of the peptide specificities (Schneider and Stephens, 1990). Manual identification and elimination of flat or non-specific logos was required when multiple specificities were identified. The PWMs are available at the following supplementary website: http://baderlab.org/Data/SH3Human.
