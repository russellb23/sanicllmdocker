The PDB contains structures of nine unique domains in complex with class | or Il peptides (Figures 2A-2C), including four human domains (LYN-1/1, FYN-1/1, GRB2-1/2 and NCK2-1/3-SV1/2) and five homologs (SRC-1/1- 1S1/2, CRK-1/2, PLCG1-1/1, FGR-1/1 and SRC-1/1-IS2/2). In most cases, the PxxP core is flanked by hydrophobic amino acids that improve van der Waals and hydrophobic interactions, and thus optimize the packing geometries of the Pro residues (Lim et al., 1994). Leu is the most prevalent flanking residue (Sar- aste and Musacchio, 1994), and the representative structure of the class I/Il domain FYN-1/1 bound to a peptide containing a ~'LP°xL*?P*5(+) motif reveals that the tandem repeats in the peptide pack against proximal and distal hydrophobic pockets formed by Y9, W34 and P46, or Y7 and Y49, respectively (Fig- ure 4A) (Camara-Artigas et al., 2016).
