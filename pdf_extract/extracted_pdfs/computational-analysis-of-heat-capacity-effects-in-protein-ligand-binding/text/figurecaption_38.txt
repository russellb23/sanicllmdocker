Figure 1. Thermodynamic process describing the calculation of binding heat capacities from four different MD_ simulations: apoenzyme, holoenzyme, free ligand in water, and pure water. For each separate system, the derivative of the total energy is calculated as a function of temperature. Black triangles schematically denote solvent molecules that are displaced by the ligand. The total number of solvent molecules is kept constant both in the upper and lower processes so that the overall process does not involve any change in the number of degrees of freedom.
