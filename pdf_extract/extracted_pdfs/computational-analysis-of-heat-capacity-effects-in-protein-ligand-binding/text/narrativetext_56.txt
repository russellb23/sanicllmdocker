The MD simulations yield essentially perfectly linear relationships between the average total potential energy (Uj) and temperature, both for the apoenzyme and for its complexes with the three inhibitors (Figure 3). Hence, the R* values for these plots are all equal to 1, to within the third decimal, and the quantity QU/0T is thus very well-defined. Moreover, this indicates that the heat capacity for each system is relatively constant over the temperature interval and does not change appreciably.
