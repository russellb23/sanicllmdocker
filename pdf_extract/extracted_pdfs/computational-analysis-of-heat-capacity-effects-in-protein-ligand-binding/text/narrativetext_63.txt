Free-Energy Perturbation Calculations. Since the three inhibitors have slightly different binding free energies and enthalpies, as measured by stopped-flow and SPR experi- ments,” it is also interesting to examine the performance of FEP calculations in this case. To this end, we calculated the binding free-energy difference (AAG,ing) between compounds 2 and 4, which only differ by one methyl group, as a function of temperature (Supporting Information). These results show that even if the predicted values of AAG,,,.3 around —0.3 kcal/ mol agree fairly well with the experimentally measured quantity (AAGing = —0.39 kcal/mol at 25 °C), the calculated temperature dependence of AAG,,,4 is not accurate enough for reliable estimation of AC, differences between ligands (Figure $1).
