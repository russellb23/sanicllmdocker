The crystal structure of the Lyn SH3 domain (Fig 1A and 1B) contains five B-strands orga- nized into two B-sheets. Previously termed B1 -B5, these B-strands are connected via four loops termed the RT loop, n-src loop, distal loop, and 3,9 helical loop. Comparison of our crys- tal structure with the structure of the unliganded Lyn SH3 domain determined by NMR spec- troscopy (PDB 1WIF) [36] yields an RMSD of the Co. atoms of 0.76 A for residues of the B- barrel and 0.93 A for all Co. atoms (Fig 2A).
