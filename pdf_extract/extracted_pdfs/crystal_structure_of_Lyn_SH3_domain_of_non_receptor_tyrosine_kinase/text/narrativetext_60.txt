Flexibility of the RT and n-src loops has previously been suggested as important for interac- tion with polyproline motifs, which might proceed via an induced fit mechanism [52-55]. These movements are expected to optimize the contacts between the SH3 domain and the polyproline motif. This could allow for variations on the polyproline sequence motifs that can selectively interact with particular SH3 domains. In the case of the Lyn kinase, physiological partners include PI-3 kinase and Ras-GAP [5, 6]. These binding partners are proposed to alter the position of the SH3 domain in the full-length Src-family kinases, which increases kinase activity.
