In the absence of a mutation or a protein-protein interaction, the SH3 domain of Src family kinases binds to the kinase domain and inhibits activity [45, 56]. Physiologically, protein-pro- tein interactions with the SH3 domain are believed to move its position so that it no longer inhibits the activity [56, 57]. Thus, the cancer-associated mutations in the Lyn SH3 domain could increase Lyn activity by eliminating this self-inhibition.
