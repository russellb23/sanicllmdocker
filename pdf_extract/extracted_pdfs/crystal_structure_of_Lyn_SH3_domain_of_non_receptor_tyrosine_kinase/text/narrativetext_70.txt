Fig 3. Cancer-associated mutations of the Lyn SH3 domain. A. Mutations of 18 out of the 61 amino acids of the Lyn SH3 domain were identified by genome sequencing of patients with the indicated types of cancer [23-25]. The table separates these mutations into likely-transformative and likely non-transformative, based upon prior computational analysis [58]. B. Locations of the likely transformative cancer-associated mutations are shown in red. Locations of the likely non-transformative substitutions are shown in green.
