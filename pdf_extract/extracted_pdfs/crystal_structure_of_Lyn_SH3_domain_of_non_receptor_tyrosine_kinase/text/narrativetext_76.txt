Advances in sequencing now allow mutations in individual cancer patients to be identified. Analysis of whole genomes revealed 18 cancer-associated point mutations within the Lyn SH3 domain, affecting ~30% of the residues in this domain [23-25] (Fig 3). While cell assays evalu- ating the impact of these single amino acid changes have not, to date, been reported, the use of computational prediction algorithms suggests that 12 of these substitutions are likely to be transformative and contribute to disease.
