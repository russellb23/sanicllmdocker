Figure S7: Convergence test using the MIST2 entropy. Horizontal axis is the length of the time interval used for entropy calculation. The first data point used 100-120 ns, the second point used 100-140 ns, etc. The last point used the whole 100-500-ns interval. (a) Backbone and (b) side-chain entropy of nSH3. (c) Backbone and (d) side-chain entropy of PRMs. For a given category, the relative difference in entropy between systems involving PRM‘*! and PRMN®! is established for time intervals much shorter than 400 ns, except for the backbone entropy of nSH3 where the relative difference starts to emerge for time intervals longer than 300 ns. However, the backbone of nSH3 contributes the least to the entropy change upon ligand binding (Table 1). Since the backbone entropy of nSH3 is similar among different systems, the vertical scale in panel (a) is narrower than those in other panels.
