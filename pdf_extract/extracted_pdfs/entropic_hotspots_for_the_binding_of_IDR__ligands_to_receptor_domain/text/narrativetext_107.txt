It is notable that the side-chain dynamics is different from the backbone dynamics. The backbone entropy loss of PRM“?! is larger than that of PRMNS!, whereas the converse holds for the side-chain entropy loss. If only the backbone order parameter or backbone entropy were considered, which has been easier to measure experimen- tally, one might conclude that PRMNS! is more flexible than PRM“?! in the bound state. It is thus essential to also consider the side-chain dynamics to establish a more complete understanding of the entropy contribution.
