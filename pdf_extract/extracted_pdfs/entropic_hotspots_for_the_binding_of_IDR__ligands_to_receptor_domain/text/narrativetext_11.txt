The proline-rich motif (PRM) is one of the most abundant linear motifs found in IDPs/IDRs (10,11). The cyclic struc- ture of proline limits conformational degrees of freedom (DOFs) (12), and PRMs tend to form left-handed polypro- line type II (PPIL) helices upon binding to its receptor (13).
