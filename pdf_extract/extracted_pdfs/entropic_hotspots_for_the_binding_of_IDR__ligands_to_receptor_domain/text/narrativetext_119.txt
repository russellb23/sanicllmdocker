We found that ligand binding induces per-residue side-chain entropy changes both at the nSH3:PRM interface and across nSH3. For residues of nSH3 that form high-occupancy contacts with PRM, side-chain entropy decreases to varying extents, the largest of which were the highly conserved and nonpolar F141 and W169.
