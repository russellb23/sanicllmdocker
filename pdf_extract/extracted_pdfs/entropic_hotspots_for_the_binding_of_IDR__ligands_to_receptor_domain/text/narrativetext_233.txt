The order parameter ranges between 0 (flexible) and 1 (rigid) (1, 2). To ensure convergence of calculated values, we calculated the angular reorientational correlation function C;(t)(3) (Eq. 2; Fig. S1). The resulting relaxation time is generally longer in flexible regions, the longest being 46.6 ns, for K189 of nSH3 in the nSH3:PRMNS!8 complex (Fig. S2a). To minimize any potential influence of the initially prepared state, we excluded the first 100 ns from our analysis.
