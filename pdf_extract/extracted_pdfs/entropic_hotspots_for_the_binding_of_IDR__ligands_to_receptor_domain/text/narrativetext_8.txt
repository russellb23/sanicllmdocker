Intrinsically disordered proteins (IDP) or proteins contain- ing intrinsically disordered regions (IDR) are abundant in cells (1), and they are widely involved in recognition be- tween proteins (2-7). Many IDPs/IDRs undergo disorder- to-order transitions upon binding to their partner proteins.
