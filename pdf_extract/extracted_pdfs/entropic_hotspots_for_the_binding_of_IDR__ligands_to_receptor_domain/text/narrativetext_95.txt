For nSH3:PRM™S'®, the D174-E176 bond had nearly the same occupancy in the complex, 55.5%, and the occupancy of K178-E176 increased to 88.1%, again in line with the side- chain entropy loss of K178. Thus, the contact occupancy and side-chain entropy changes upon complex formation are somewhat correlated when the occupancy increases to a high value, which may be due to a restriction in side-chain motion. However, two residues forming a high-occupancy contact do not experience comparable side-chain entropy loss, as seen for E176.
