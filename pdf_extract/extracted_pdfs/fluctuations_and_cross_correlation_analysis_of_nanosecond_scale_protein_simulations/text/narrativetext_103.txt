Fitting the structure of a reference conformation in order to remove rigid-body motion is a necessary initial procedure when performing fluctuation analysis. Translational and rotational diffusion of the protein would otherwise result in spurious covari- ances between atom pairs and, over long simulation times, the (interesting) internal fluctuations would be biased or disappear in the noise. There is unfortunately no unique procedure for performing a “physically meaningful’ fit. It may seem better to perform the fitting with respect to backbone atoms rather than side-chain atoms, because the former are less subject to random fluctuations, but such choices are subjective.
