The crystal structure of BPTI was taken from the entry APTI (Wlodawer et al., 1987) of the Brookhaven Protein Data Bank (Bernstein et al., 1977). The protein was placed in a truncated octahedron box corresponding to a cube of edge length 5.40 nm containing 2371 extended simple point charge (SPC/E) water molecules (Berendsen et al., 1987) resulting in a total of 7717 atoms. The system was equilibrated during 10 ps at 277K using the standard GROMOS87 force field. The aromatic hydrogen atoms were then generated and the system was allowed to relax for a further 40 ps at 300 K with the improved force field parameters.
