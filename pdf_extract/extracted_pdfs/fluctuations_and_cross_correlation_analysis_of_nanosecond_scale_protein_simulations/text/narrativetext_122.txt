The crystal structure of triclinic HEWL (Ramanadham et al., 1987), entry 2LZT of the Brookhaven Protein Data Bank, served as a starting structure for the lysozyme simulation. The protein was placed in a truncated octahedron box corresponding to a cube of edge length 6.71 nm containing 4463 simple point charge (SPC) water molecules (Berendsen et al., 1981), resulting in a total of 14,710 atoms. The system was equilibrated during 50 ps with progressive relaxation of positional restraints and regular increase in the temperature from 250 K to 300 K.
