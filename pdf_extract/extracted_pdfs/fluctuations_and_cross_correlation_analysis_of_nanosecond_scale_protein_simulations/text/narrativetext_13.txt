Atomic positional fluctuation and cross-corre- lation analysis techniques seem appealing for two reasons. First, atomic positional fluctuations can formally be related to crystallographic temperature factors or B-factors, and thus compared with available experimental data. Second, cross-corre- lation analysis can be used to broaden the scope or time-scale of a MD simulation by extrapolating the motions along directions in which major changes should occur, for example on a longer time-scale or upon substrate binding, without actually simulat- ing these motions.
