In Figure 5c, the time development of the cross-correlations between the positional fluctu- ations of selected atom pairs belonging to the residues surrounding disulfide bridge (5-55) linking the two previously described regions, are displayed. The behavior shown is typical for most of the correlations between pairs of atoms within the protein. Erratic behavior is observed between 0 and about 200 ps, with 37% of the 1711 C*-C* correlations changing sign at least once between 50 and 200 ps. Absolute values of the cross-correlation coefficients remain relatively low. From this time-point on, cross-correlations begin to diverge and after about 500 ps are converging towards their nanosecond values.
