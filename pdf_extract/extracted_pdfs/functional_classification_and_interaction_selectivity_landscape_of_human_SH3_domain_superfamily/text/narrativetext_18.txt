Protein—protein interactions are fundamental to the intricate machinery that controls virtually all biological processes. [1]. Among the diverse array of protein domains that facilitate these interactions, the SRC homology 3 (SH3) domains stand out as central modular units. These compact domains, consisting of approximately 60 amino acids with similar sequences that adopt a compact B-barrel fold made of five f-strand [2], are found predominantly in various signaling proteins and various protein families [3,4]. The selective interactions of the SH3 domain with proline-rich motifs (PRMs) are fundamental for the assembly and orchestration of multiprotein complexes [5].
