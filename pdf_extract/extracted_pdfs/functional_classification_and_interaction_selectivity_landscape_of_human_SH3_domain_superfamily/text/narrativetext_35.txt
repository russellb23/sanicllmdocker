Pull-down of 10 uM of FITC-labeled peptides (Table S2) with 5 uM of purified GST- SH3 domains (Table S1) was performed using 10 pL of glutathione Sepharose beads (GE Healthcare, Chalfont Saint Giles, UK) in a buffer containing 30 mM Tris-HCl at pH 7.5, 3 mM dithiothreitol, and 5 mM MgCl? for 1 h at 4 °C. Purified GST was used as a negative control. After three washes, bound proteins were eluted by incubation in the same buffer containing 20 mM reduced glutathione for 15 min at 4 °C, and the beads were separated by centrifugation. Bound FITC-labeled peptides were detected by dot blot analysis using 1 uL of eluent at an emission wavelength of 600 nm and an Odyssey Fe imaging system (LI-COR Biosciences, Lincoln, NE, USA). Detected signals were quantified densitometrically using LI-COR Image Studio version 5.2 imaging software.
