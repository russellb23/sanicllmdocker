DENND1A that showed 71% selection of CrkL SH3 in phage screening also preferentially associated with CrkL in this pull-down compar- ison, although its selectivity at the 500 nu SH3 concentration ested was less striking than observed for SH3YL1 and SH2B. However, considering that DENND1A does not contain a pep- ide showing even intermediate level binding to CrkL SH3, this agreement with the preference of native DENND1A for CrkL observed in phage screening is remarkable. Finally, PHF21B hat preferred Src as well as Fyn SH3 domain (both 43% of selected clones) in library screens, also bound to these two SH3 domains more strongly than to Lyn or CrkL. In conclu- sion, the results obtained in this pull-down assay including our SH3 ligand proteins versus four selected SH3 domains correlated well with the binding preferences identified for hese native ligands, but agreed poorly with the peptide array data, and in some cases (for example PHF21B) even con- rasted the SH3 binding profiles of isolated proline-rich pep- ides from the same proteins.
