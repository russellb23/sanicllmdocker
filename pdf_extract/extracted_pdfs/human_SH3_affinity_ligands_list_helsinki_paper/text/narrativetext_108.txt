To estimate the binding affinity involved in the dominant SHS - ligand partnerships revealed by the SHS library screens we chose two such interactions for more detailed analyses. One was binding of HCLS1 to Lyn-SH3, which similar to the SH2B/Lyn interaction was suggested to be of high affinity by the exclusive affinity selection of Lyn-SH3 by HCLS1 from the phage library. The other one was the SH3YL1/Src-SH@ inter- action, which appeared to be particularly strong when exam- ined for its selectivity in Fig. 3B. Dilution series of Lyn and Src SH3 domains were incubated with beads coated with their cognate ligand proteins, and their association with HCLS1 and SH3YL1 was examined with the pull-down assay de- scribed above (Figs. 3F and 3G). When the capacity of HCLS1 and SH3YL1 to capture Lyn and Src SH3 domains provided at different concentrations was quantified and examined by Scatchard analysis (Fig. 3H), submicromolar affinity values indicative of exceptionally tight SH3 binding could be deter- mined. The Kp value of the HCLS1/Lyn-SH3@ interaction was estimated to be 746 nm, whereas an affinity as high as 187 nu was found for the SH3YL1/Src-SH3 complex. By contrast, when binding of HCLS1 to Fyn-SH3 was tested an affinity of only 7 wm was measured (supplemental Fig. S1).
