In this study we have made use of a phage display library that contains a near complete collection of human SH3 do- mains to carry out a large-scale protein interaction screen involving hundreds of potential human SHS ligand proteins. A key objective of our study was to systematically examine how commonly strong and selective interactions occur among the human “SH interactome.” In addition, identification of such robust SH3 interactions could provide valuable new leads for research aimed at unraveling signaling protein networks that regulate cell behavior.
