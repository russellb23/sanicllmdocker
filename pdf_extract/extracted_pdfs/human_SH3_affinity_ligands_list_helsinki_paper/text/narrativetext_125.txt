On the other hand, despite forming a minority, our current data show that SH interactions with a high affinity and spec- ificity do exist at a reasonable frequency. Using a semiquan- titative interaction assay we could establish submicromolar affinities and a remarkably high selectivity in binding even to closely related SH3 domains for the dominant SH3 phage library-discovered interactions that we examined. Thus, our data suggest that in some instances SH3 binding could also play a major role in determining protein interaction partner- ships, and in driving the assembly of specific protein com- plexes in cells.
