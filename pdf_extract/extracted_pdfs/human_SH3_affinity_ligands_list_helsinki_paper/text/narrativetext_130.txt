Thus, we conclude that the SH3 selectivity of the 19 native protein ligands examined in this study is either based on binding determinants that are located outside of the core SH3 binding motifs contained in the arrayed peptides, or alterna- tively, are dependent on a particular conformation of the peptide that is lost when it is no longer presented as a part of the corresponding folded protein. In either case, our data suggest that large-scale peptide arrays screens have rela- tively limited value for attempts to characterize relevant SH3- mediated protein interaction networks in cells.
