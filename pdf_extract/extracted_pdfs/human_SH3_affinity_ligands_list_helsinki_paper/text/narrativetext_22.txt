The pEBB/PP vector is driven by the elon- gation factor 1a (EF-1a) promoter, and contains a 123 amino acids long biotin acceptor domain (19), to which we will refer as the “PP domain” in the presented study. Recombination (Gateway LR) reac- tions between pEBB/PP-DEST and individual ORFs were performed by using Gateway LR Clonase II Enzyme Mix (Invitrogen, Carlsbad, CA) according to manufacturer’s recommendations. Each of the re- sultant pEBB/PP-ORF constructs encoded the desired ORF in frame with PP domain sequence at the N terminus.
