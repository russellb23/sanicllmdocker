The 449 selected ORFs were subjected to subcloning into a Gateway-compatible destination vector pEBB/PP-DEST gen- erated for this purpose (see Experimental Procedures). pEBB/ PP-DEST is driven by the elongation factor 1 (EF-1a) pro- moter, and contains a 123 amino acids long biotin acceptor domain (“PP domain”; (19)) plus Gateway destination se- quence attR1-ccdB-attR2 for recombination with ORF-en- coding sequences and bacterial selection of recombinant clones. The putative ligand proteins were transiently ex- pressed as biotinylated PP-fusion proteins in human embry- onic kidney 293T cells (HEK293T), and immobilized onto streptavidin-coated paramagnetic beads for a single round affinity panning of the SH3 phage library.
