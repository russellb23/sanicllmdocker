Fic. 1. Schematic outline of the experimental strategy used. Biotinylation domain (PP)-tagged putative ligand proteins were ex- pressed by transient transfection, and immobilized onto streptavidin- coated paramagnetic beads. Subsequently a comprehensive library of human SH3 domain-expressing phages (296 different ones) was incubated with the potential ligand, followed by washing off the unbound phages, and infection of bacteria with the captured phages, and identification of these SH3 clones by DNA sequencing.
