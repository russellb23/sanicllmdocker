On the other hand, several ligand proteins that showed strong selection of individual SH3 domains from the phage library did not contain a peptide capable of higher than aver- age binding to these SH3 domains. A striking example of this was PHF21B that preferentially selected Fyn and Src SH3 domains as its partners from the phage library. PHF21B con- tains a single proline-rich motif (DSLPVAPGRDRPPKQPPT) that in the peptide array showed strong (almost five times the average) and highly selective binding to CMS SHS (1/3), but was negative for Fyn and Src.
