Figure 3. Effect of proline-to-alanine mutation on the binding mode of PRM. Overlapped structures of (top) unbound peptides and (bottom) bound peptides aligned with respect to SH3 of (a) PRM P8A, (b) PRM P11A, and (c) PRM P8AP11A. Proteins and peptides are colored blue and red, respectively. For a protein structure, only one averaged structure was used for a visualization of the protein structure while 100 structures of peptides from trajectories were sampled after the alignment of proteins. The alignment reference is indicated in parentheses. (d) Contact map and (e) its difference of PRM P8A. Contact map difference was calculated by subtracting the contact map of each WT from that of PRM P8A.
