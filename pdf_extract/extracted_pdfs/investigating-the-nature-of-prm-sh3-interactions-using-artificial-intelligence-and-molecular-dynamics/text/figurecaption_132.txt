Figure 5. Effect of the proline-to-alanine mutation on the binding mode of PRM(H). Overlapped structures: (top) unbound peptides and (bottom) bound peptides aligned with respect to SH3 of (a) PRM(H) WT, (b) PRM(H) P7A, (c) PRM(H) P9A, and (d) PRM(H) P7AP9A. Proteins and peptides are colored blue and red, respectively. For a protein structure, only one averaged structure was used for a visualization of the protein structure while 100 structures of peptides from trajectories were sampled after the alignment of proteins. The alignment reference is indicated in parentheses. (e) Intrapeptide interaction energy difference defined by subtracting the intrapeptide interaction energy of the peptides in bound states from that in unbound states. (f) Intrapeptide distance maps in bound states of PRM(H) WT. Intrapeptide distance map difference in bound states of (g) PRM(H) P7A, (h) PRM(H) P9A, and (i) PRM(H) P7AP9A. Intrapeptide distance map difference was calculated by subtracting the intrapeptide distance map of PRM(H) WT from that of each protein—peptide complex.
