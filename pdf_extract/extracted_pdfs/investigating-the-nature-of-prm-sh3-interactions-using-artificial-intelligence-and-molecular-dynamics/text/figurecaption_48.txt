Figure 1. Binding nature of the PRM WT. (a) Overlapped structures of (left) unbound peptides, (middle) bound peptides aligned with respect to SH3, and (right) bound peptides aligned with respect to PRM. Proteins and peptides are colored blue and red, respectively. For a protein structure in bound peptide structures aligned with respect to SH3, only one averaged SH3 was used for a visualization of the protein structure while 100 structures of protein in bound peptides aligned with respect to PRM were used for that. All images have 100 structures of peptides after the alignment. (b) RMSD of peptides in unbound peptides, bound peptides aligned with respect to SH3, and bound peptides aligned with respect to PRM. The alignment reference is indicated in parentheses. (c) Contact map of PRM WT where (1), (2), and (3) indicate the main contact regions. (d) Ramachandran plots of PRM WT. The number in front of each amino acid corresponds to those of contact maps in panel (c). To avoid infinity in the logarithm calculation, the empty bins in the 2D histogram were filled by 0.5.
