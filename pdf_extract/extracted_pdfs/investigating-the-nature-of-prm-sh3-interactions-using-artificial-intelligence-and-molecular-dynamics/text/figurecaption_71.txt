Figure 2. Effect of charged mutations on the binding mode of PRM. Overlapped structures of (left) unbound peptides and (right) bound peptides aligned with respect to SH3 of (a) PRM +/— and (b) PRM +/0. The alignment reference is indicated in parentheses. Proteins and peptides are colored blue and red, respectively. For a protein structure, only one averaged structure was used for a visualization of the protein structure, while 100 structures peptides from trajectories were sampled after the alignment of proteins. Contact map differences of (c) PRM +/— and (d) PRM +/0. Contact map difference was calculated by subtracting the contact map of PRM WT from that of each protein—peptide complex.
