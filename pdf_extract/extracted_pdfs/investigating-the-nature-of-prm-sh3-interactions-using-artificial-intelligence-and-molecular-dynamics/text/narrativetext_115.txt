3.4. PRM(H) Systems. We extended our analysis to PRM(H) systems. The binding affinities of PRM(H) WT and its proline-to-alanine mutants were experimentally determined using isothermal titration calorimetry (ITC) and were found to be notably stronger than those of the PRM WT motifs.'> Unlike in the PRM systems, the strong binding of the peptide to SH3 in PRM(H) does not diminish even after proline-to-alanine mutations. Figure Sa—d shows the binding structures of PRM(H)-based protein—peptide complexes. In all complexes, the peptides are firmly bound to the protein, adopting dumbbell- like shapes; the middle of the peptide is strongly bound and the termini fluctuate. Importantly, the contact mode, dispersion, and distance map of PRM(H) change marginally upon the mutations (Figures $4, S6, and S7).
