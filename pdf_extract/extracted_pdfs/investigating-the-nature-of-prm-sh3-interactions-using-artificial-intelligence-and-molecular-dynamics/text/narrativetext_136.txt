As fluctuations are related to the transient interactions, it should be noted that our measures cannot explain the cases involving a change in strong interactions. For example, PRM WT and PRM +/0 have different natures of interactions; the former is dominated by electrostatic interactions, but the latter lacks the positive charges. In this case, the nature of the N- terminal-SH3 interaction has totally changed, and our measures are incapable of detecting this change. A more delicate case is the PRM(H) series. As we discussed above, their interactions are an intricate combination of strong interactions and transient interactions, and it is not easy to disentangle one from the other.
