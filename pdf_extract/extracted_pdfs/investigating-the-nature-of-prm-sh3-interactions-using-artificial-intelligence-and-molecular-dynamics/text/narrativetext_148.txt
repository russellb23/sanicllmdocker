Using AlphaFold2 and molecular dynamics simulations, we discovered that proline-to-alanine mutations enhance flexibility, leading to a reduction in binding affinity, while charge-altering mutations modify both the binding mode and strength in PRM:SH3 systems. The PRM(H) series demonstrated that binding is primarily driven by local flexibility and the hydrophobic effect. Additionally, we found a correlation between the root-mean-square deviation (RMSD) and dendro- gram height with experimental dissociation constants.
