ABSTRACT: Understanding the binding interactions within protein—peptide complexes is crucial for elucidating key physicochemical phenomena in biological systems. Among the outcomes of these interactions, biomolecular condensates have recently emerged as vital players in various cellular functions including signaling. Complexes such as PRM:SH3 are known to undergo condensation, yet the chemical interactions and governing factors driving these behaviors remain poorly understood.
