According to an editorial on data sharing and reproducibility of scientific results, all files related to AlphaFold2, including multiple sequence alignment (MSA) data, MSA coverage, predicted alignment errors, predicted contacts, predicted distograms, predicted local distance difference test (LDDT), structures from ranks 1 to 5, input sequences, and parameter files, have been deposited on GitHub at https://github.com/ sejunkim6370/AlphaFold2-and-Molecular-Dynamics. Addi- tionally, molecular dynamics data, including solvated structure files, input and parameter files for energy minimization, NVT simulation, NPT simulation, and production MD simulation, as well as ion input and parameter files, are also available. Furthermore, the topology files for the system, SH3, and the PRM or PRM(H) series, along with their auxiliary topology files for restraints, are stored on the same GitHub repository. For all simulations, we used GROMACS 2020.3, which is available under the appropriate legal license at https://www.gromac- s.org/. The CHARMM36m force field (March 2019) was employed for all simulations, and it can be accessed without restrictions at https://github.com/sejunkim6370/AlphaFold2- and-Molecular-Dynamics or downloaded from https://mack- erell.umaryland.edu/charmm _ff.shtml#gromacs.
