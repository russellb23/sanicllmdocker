Notably, the PRM(H) series shows that binding is primarily driven by local flexibility and the hydrophobic effect. Furthermore, we demonstrate that the root-mean-square deviation and dendrogram height are correlated to experimental dissociation constants. These insights provide a framework for understanding the binding behaviors of protein—peptide complexes and offer an effective approach for studying similar systems.
