In the emerging field of biomolecular condensation, under- standing protein—peptide interactions provides valuable in- sights into the molecular mechanisms underlying condensation. Interactions between multivalent biomolecules can form transient networks, leading to biomolecular condensation both in vitro and in vivo.”° One of the early model systems involves interaction between the Src homology 3 (SH3) domain and the proline-rich motif (PRM) peptide. The tandem repeats of SH3 and PRM can undergo condensation when mixed in vitro.”* Another notable example is Rubisco condensation in the pyrenoid, where interactions between the multivalent Rubisco enzyme and the essential pyrenoid component 1 (EPYC1) peptide play a crucial role.”'°
