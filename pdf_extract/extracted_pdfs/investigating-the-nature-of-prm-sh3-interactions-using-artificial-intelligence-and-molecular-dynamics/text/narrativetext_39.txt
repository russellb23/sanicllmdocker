To generate all initial structures, we used ColabFold,”' which enables fast prediction of protein complexes within Many- against-Many sequence searching (MMseqs2)*””* and Alpha- Fold2”° framework, based on the sequences of the SH3 protein and PRM peptide provided in ref 15 (Table 1). For efficient simulations of the protein—peptide complexes, we removed the tails of the SH3 protein, as these regions remained disordered even after simulations with the full sequences and apparently did not contribute to the binding mode. Using these truncated sequences, we predicted the initial structures from AlphaFold2 and selected the top-ranked structures as the initial models for MD simulations. For the PRM(H) P7AP9A, however, we chose the second-ranked structure because a significantly different binding structure was predicted for the first-ranked structure (Figure S1). The structures of the unbound PRM or PRM(H) peptides were generated by using only their sequence information and AlphaFold2.
