To obtain contact maps and distance maps, we calculated the inter-residue distances using the positions of the a carbon atoms and set the contact cutoff at 1 nm. Trajectory analysis was performed using MDTraj.°° Potential of mean force (PME) in Ramachandran plots was calculated by applying the natural logarithm to the populations in the plot and then multiplying the logarithm of the populations by —1. For the zero bin, we assigned a value of 0.5 to avoid issues when applying the natural logarithm. Pairwise RMSD was calculated by pairing each frame with every other frame, aligning one structure to another, and then computing the RMSD. This produces a square matrix of RMSD values, where the dimensions correspond to the square of the number of frames in the trajectory.
