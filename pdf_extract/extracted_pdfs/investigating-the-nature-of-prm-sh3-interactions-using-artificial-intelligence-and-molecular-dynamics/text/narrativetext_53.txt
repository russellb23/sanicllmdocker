We used a contact frequency map to quantitatively investigate the interface between the protein and peptide (Figure Ic). Three main contact regions were identified: (1) PRM residues 6—10 with SH3 residues 7—10, (2) PRM residues 10—14 with SH3 residues 34—36, and (3) PRM residues 7—13 with SH3 residues 48—52. Region 1 is extended to include transient interactions between the N-terminus of PRM and SH3 residues 8—13. Interestingly, the electrostatic interactions between acidic and basic residues are not always significant, particularly between PRM residues 1—5 and SH3 residues 13-17.
