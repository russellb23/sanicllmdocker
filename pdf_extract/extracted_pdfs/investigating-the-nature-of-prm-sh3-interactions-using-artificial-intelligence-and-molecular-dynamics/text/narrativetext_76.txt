The structural ensembles of PRM +/— and PRM +/0 are shown in Figure 2a and Figure 2b, respectively. The R, values indicate that binding does not significantly affect the chain dimensions (Figures S2 and $3). We also obtained their contact maps (Figure $4) and compared them with the contact map of PRM WT (Figure 2¢d). They both show globally similar patterns. The electrostatic interactions between the KKKK region of PRM (residues 2—S) and the acidic tract of SH3 (residues 13-17) are diminished, while interactions between the mutated region of PRM and residues 7 and 8 of SH3 are enhanced. This can be explained by the synergistic effect of electrostatic interactions between the glutamates of PRM (Glu2 and Glu4) and the lysine of SH3 (Lys7) and hydrophobic interactions between the hydrophobic residues of PRM (phenylalanine and isoleucine) and the phenylalanine of SH3 (Phe8). These new interactions are stronger, resulting in a conformational change that stretches the peptide.
