Fig. 2. Energy network representation of the conformational transitions of protein dynamics. An illustrative energy landscape is shown as a 3D image (center) and as a contour plot (left). On the right its transformation to an energy network is described. In the energy network representation (right) nodes represent local energy minima, while solid and dotted lines denote strong and weak links representing low and high activation energy transitions between two local energy minima, respectively. The rectangle on the bottom right of the network represents the lowest energy state to mark the native state of the respective protein.
