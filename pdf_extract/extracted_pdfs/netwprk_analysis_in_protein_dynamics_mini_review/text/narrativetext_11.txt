We must warn that the above summary of the major features of self-organizing, real-world networks is largely a generaliza- tion, which is often not observed in its pure form. Real-world networks are often heterogeneous, and their different modules may behave completely differently. Moreover, sampling bias and improper data analysis may show the above features in such cases, where they do not actually exist. Therefore, special caution has to be taken to scrutinize the validity and extent of datasets, use correct sampling procedures and adequate meth- ods of data analysis [8-11].
