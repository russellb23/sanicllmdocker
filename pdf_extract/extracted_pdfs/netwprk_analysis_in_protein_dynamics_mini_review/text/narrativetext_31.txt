As an exception from most self-organized networks, the de- gree distribution of protein structure networks seems to be Poissonian and not scale-free [17,18]. The Poissonian degree distribution means that protein structures have a much smaller number of hubs than most self-organized networks including most cellular or social networks. The major reason for this deviation from the scale-free degree distribution lies in the lim- ited simultaneous binding capacity of a given amino acid side- chain (also called as excluded volume effect).
