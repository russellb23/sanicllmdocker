Domains usually fold separately, have a function and are conserved during evolution. The distribution of the folds of various domains follows a scale-free pattern [32] meaning that there is a small number of very ‘popular’, stable folds, and we have a relatively big number of unique, orphan folds. The underlying reason of the ‘popular’ folds is evolutionary selec- tion, which preferred those structures, which are both stable and fold easily. These structures are the ones, which have the common feature of the small-worldness and the other topolog- ical specialties, which were either mentioned above, or will be detailed further in Section 5.
