However, the disorder of protein structure is a matter of time-scale, and is much more prevalent than it is thought to the first glance. Flexibility of the polypeptide-chain leads to structural fluctuations [34]. However, this ‘short-term’ disorder is caused by fluctuations around an equilibrium con- formation, which is different from the lack of equilibrium con- formation observed in unstructured protein regions.
