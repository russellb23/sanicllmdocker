The early work of Ansari et al. [35] already showed the exis- tence of ‘protein-quakes’, i.e. the cascading relaxation ava- lanche of myoglobin after the photodissociation of carbon monoxide. A number of protein kinetics, including the above- mentioned carbon monoxide dissociation, enzyme actions, ex- change of protein protons to those of water and protein folding, are similar to Levy-flights, and show a scale-free statis- tics in the time-gaps between elementary conformational changes as well as in the magnitude of these changes [36-38]. Scale-free distributions and avalanches resemble to the behav- ior in ‘self-organized criticality’, and are typical features of sys- tems with restricted relaxation [4]. In proteins the restrictions come from the necessity to break bonds in large-scale confor- mational transitions, which can be called as a local unfolding event. However, most protein motions (such as those observed after ligand binding) do not require bond-rearrangements and can be well approximated by quasi-harmonic dynamic [39].
