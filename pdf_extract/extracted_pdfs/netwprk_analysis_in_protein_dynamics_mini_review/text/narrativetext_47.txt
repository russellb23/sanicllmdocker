In most conformational rearrangements the above scale-free distributions become more complex, which is due to the hier- archical and modular structure of the underlying protein struc- tural network. In these real scenarios we observe the integration of the correlated scale-free distributions of the indi- vidual, overlapping network modules [36-38]. As an example of the inter-modular correlation of protein dynamics, Balog et al. [29] recently showed that conformational transitions of the individual domains are not additive in the simulation of phosphoglycerate kinase dynamics. Correlated motions of a network of distant residues have also been observed in dihy- drofolate reductase [40].
