Protein dynamics can also be assessed by analyzing the propagation of perturbations in the hydrogen-bond network of the protein. A simplified, 2D network representation of hydrogen bonds, called HB-plot already revealed a number of key features of protein dynamics in the examples of cyto- chrome P450 and ligand-gated ion channels [24].
