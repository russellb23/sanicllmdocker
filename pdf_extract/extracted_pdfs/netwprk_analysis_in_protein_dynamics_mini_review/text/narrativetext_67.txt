Modularization of the energy network may also help us to solve the basic dilemma of the definition of energy networks, ie. “What may we regard as a local energy minimum of the underlying energy landscape?” Local minima are by far not only sharp, well-defined topological features of the energy landscape. Many times local minima may form or may tempo- rarily expand to shallow local basins with numerous fluctuat- ing ‘real’ minima inside. Therefore, a more exact approach is to take all possible conformations as a ‘local minimum’ and determine the basins as primary modules of the resulting hier- archical networks.
