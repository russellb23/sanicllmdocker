S. cerevisiae growth conditions. Cells were grown with their specific selection antibiotics in YPD medium (1% yeast extract, 2% tryptone, 2% glucose, and 2% agar (for solid medium)) and with the combination of NAT and HPH for diploid selection. Synthetic medium (0.67% yeast nitrogen base without amino acids and without ammonium sulfate, 2% glucose, 2.5% noble agar, drop-out without ade- nine, methionine, and lysine, and 200 ug/ml methotrexate (MTX, Bioshop Canada) diluted in dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO, Bioshop Canada)) was used for the DHFR- PCA experiments (PCA selection). For WB experiments, cells were grown in a synthetic complete medium (0.174% yeast nitrogen base without amino acids, without ammonium sulfate, 2% glucose, 0.134% amino acid dropout complete, 0.5% ammonium sulfate). Slal-GFP strains were grown in a synthetic complete medium without tryptophan to reduce background fluorescence. Cells were grown in a synthetic complete medium with monosodium glutamate (0.1% MSG) instead of ammonium sulfate in hygromycin B growth experiments. See Supplementary Table 3 for all the growth media used.
