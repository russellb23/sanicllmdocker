For the hygromycin B resistance assays in liquid cultures, the ABP1 SH3 swapped strains including the ABP] SH3-deleted and ABP KO strain were grown overnight at 30°C as precultures in synthetic complete medium with MSG. The next day, OD was measured with a TECAN Infinite F200 Pro plate reader, and the cultures were diluted to OD of 1.0 in sterile nanopure water. The different strains were further diluted at 0.1 OD by adding 25 ul of cultures to 225 pl of synthetic complete medium with MSG or with MSG and Hygromycin B 120 pg/mL in 96 wells plate (final concentration of 108 pg/mL). Cells were incubated in a TECAN Infinite M Nano plate reader at 37 °C for 24h and OD was measured every 15 min.
