Cellular lysis and protein immunoprecipitation. Precultures of yeast cells expressing baits fused with the DHER F[1,2]-FLAG were grown overnight at 30°C with agitation in synthetic complete medium. The next day, cells were expanded in synthetic complete medium to reach their exponential phase (OD600 of around 1.0), pelleted, and frozen at —80°C. The equivalent of fifty OD600 of cells was resuspended in ice-cold yeast lysis buffer (10 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.4, 150 mM NaCl, 0.5mM EDTA (Millipore Sigma), 1% Triton X-100 (Millipore Sigma), and one cOmplete Mini Protease Inhibitor Cocktail (Millipore Sigma) per 10 mL) with 425-600 M glass beads (Millipore Sigma) and disrupted at 4°C by 10 cycles of 2 min vortexing and 2 min cooldown. The equivalent of 100 OD600 of lysed cells was used for the immunoprecipitation of the bait proteins using FLAG-M2 agarose beads (Millipore Sigma). The beads were incubated with the cell lysates for 120 min at 4°C with a slow rotation, followed by three washes with the yeast lysis buffer.
