Each cell time frame of the fluorescence images was processed through a python-based single-particle tracking tool—Trackpy®”. Trackpy detected the locations of Slal-GFP positive foci in each frame and linked them together to provide particle-wise trajectories. Trajectories detected in less than ten frames were considered spurious and were filtered out. Segments of the trajectories spuriously indicating movement of the particles back to the cell membrane were trimmed. The preprocessed trajectories of the particles were then used for the calculation of distances using the spatial distance module of “SciPy”6?.
