MS data analysis with SAINTexpress. SAINTexpress version 3.6.17° was used as a statistical tool to calculate the probability value of each potential protein-protein interaction compared to background contaminants using default parameters. The four control samples were used in uncompressed mode. Two unique peptide ions and a minimum iProphet probability of 0.95 were required for protein identifi- cation prior to SAINTexpress in DDA mode while only two unique peptides were required in DIA mode.
