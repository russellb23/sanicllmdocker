Few positions specifically affecting the Abp1-Hua2 SH3- dependent interaction are conserved between Abpl SH3 and other yeast SH3s; this could explain why none of the other SH3s can complement its loss upon domain swapping (Figs. 2B and 3B). A subset of mutations at positions 15 and 52, both predicted to be in contact with a target peptide, specifically strengthen the Abp1-Hua2 interaction, and could in principle lead to a higher affinity (Fig. 3B, C and Supplementary Fig. 3H).
