Significant differences in PPIs and growth phenotypes are dependent on Slal SH3 domains (Fig. 1B, F). We constructed all possible domain-position permutations within Slal (i.e., domain shuffling, Fig. 4A). Slal PPIs are highly dependent on SH3 positions (Fig. 4B and Supplementary Fig. 5A, B), which weakly correlates with Slal level of expression (Supplementary Fig. 5C, D). As Slal SH3-1 and SH3-2 bind to the same peptide motifs in vitro!4, we expected little impact from exchanging their position if peptide recognition was the sole determinant of SH3 specificity in vivo. Surprisingly, shuffling the first two domains (2|1|3) results in the loss of ~80% of Slal PPIs (Supplementary Fig. 5A).
