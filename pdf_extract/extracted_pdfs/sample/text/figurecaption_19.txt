FIG. 1. Schematic diagrams illustrating PPII helices formed by a Sre SH3 ligand (A) and an Abl SH3 ligand (B) at the binding site of Src SH3 and Abl SH3. Panel A is modified slightly from a figure that appeared in reference 6. Panel B is based on panel A and the structural analysis of Abl SH3 complexed with a 3BP-1 peptide (10). An incomplete Abl SH3 ligand (YPPPPIP) selected from the phage display library (17) is used in panel B, since the additional amino acids (three prolines) N terminal to the Tyr at position —3 may adopt an extended conformation other than the PPII helix conformation as suggested by Musacchio et al. (10).
