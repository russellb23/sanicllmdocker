Using a tryptophan fluorescence assay, we quantitatively compared the affinities of wild-type and mutant Src SH3 and Ab! SH3 for a defined set of four peptide ligands which varied in the amino acids at the —3 and —1 positions upstream of the PXXP motif (Table 1). We found that Src SH3 prefers an Arg_, since substitution of the Arg with a Tyr caused an eightfold decrease in affinity. The preference of an Arg_, can be attributed to Asp-99, since the D99N mutation lowered the affinity of the RPL peptide for Src SH3 by 37-fold. Abl SH3, on the other hand, prefers a Tyr_3, since substitution of the Tyr with an Arg also lowered the peptide affinity by eightfold. Abl Thr-98 appears to be important for this preference, since the T98D mutation lowered the affinity of Abl SH3 for the YPP peptide by 12-fold.
