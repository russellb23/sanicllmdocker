Analysis of the structures of Src, Fyn, PI3K, and Abl SH3 domains complexed with their peptide ligands revealed that the ligands adopt a left-handed type II polyproline (PPI) helix that intercalates into the binding pockets formed on the sur- face of the SH3 domains (6, 10, 26). For example, the PPII helix formed by a class I Sre SH3 ligand, R_;A_,L_, PoP, L2P3R,4Y3, interacts with three binding pockets on Src SH3 (Fig. 1A). The first binding site (formed by Tyr-90 and Tyr-136 of Src) interacts with P, and L, of the ligand. The second one is formed by the highly conserved aromatic resi- dues (Tyr-92, Trp-118, Pro-133, and Tyr-136) that make exten- sive hydrophobic contacts with L_, and P, of the ligand. The third one (positioned between Trp-118 and Asp-99) is an ar- ginine binding site in which Asp-99 appears to interact with Arg_; via a salt bridge. Asp-99 is also implicated in ligand selection, since all class I Src SH3 ligands selected from pep- tide and phage display libraries contain an Arg N terminal to the Pro-X-X-Pro motif. Furthermore, all of the SH3 domains that contain an acidic residue analogous to Sre Asp-99 (includ- ing PI3K, Fyn, and Lyn) preferentially select sequences with an Arg at the same position in class I ligands (17).
