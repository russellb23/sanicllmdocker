Although all of the Src SH3 peptide ligands selected from either phage display or peptide libraries contain an Arg_, that appears to interact with Src Asp-99 via a salt bridge, some of the natural SH3 recognition motifs identified in cellular pro- teins that bind to Src SH3 lack this analogous arginine for such an interaction (e.g., paxillin, Shc, and AFAP). To investigate the potential importance of Src Asp-99 in binding to cellular proteins and peptide ligands, we substituted Src Asp-99 with an Asn (D99N) and compared the binding properties of the mu- tant and wild-type SH3 domains. We also analyzed two Abl SH3 mutants that were engineered to contain an Asp substi- tution for Thr-98, the residue analogous to Sre Asp-99.
