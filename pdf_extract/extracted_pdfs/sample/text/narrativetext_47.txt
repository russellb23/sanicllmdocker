FIG. 3. Tyrosine phosphorylation of the SH3-binding proteins. SRD 3T3 cells were lysed with RIPA buffer and incubated with glutathione-agarose bound with GST (lane 1), GST-Sre SH3 (lane 2), GST-Sre SH3 D99N (lane 3), GST- AbI SH3 (lane 4), GST-Abl SH3 F91Y/T98D (lane 5), or GST-Abl SH3 T98D (lane 6) as described in Materials and Methods. The bound proteins were eluted with SDS sample buffer, fractionated on an 8% polyacrylamide gel, transferred to nitrocellulose, and probed with the phosphotyrosine monoclonal antibody 4G10.
