Wild-type Src SH3 selected ligands with the consensus se- quence XXXRPLPPIP from the X,PPIP library. The D99N mutation did not affect selection at position —1 or —2 but completely eliminated selection of Arg at position —3. Instead, Pro, Tyr, or Ile was selected at this position. In addition, the D99N mutation appears to impose greater selectivity at posi- tions —4, —5, and —6 than that detected for wild-type Src. All D99N-selected phage sequences, except the mutated sequence which lost the M13 gene III invariant glutamic acid residue, contained an Arg at position —4, —5, or —6.
