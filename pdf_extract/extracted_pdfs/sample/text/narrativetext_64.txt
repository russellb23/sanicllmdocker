Wild-type Abl SH3 showed a preference for the sequence PPPYPPPPIP, with Tyr selected at position —3 in 100% of phages examined. The Abl SH3 T98D mutant did not alter selection of Tyr at position —3 but did eliminate selection of Pro at position —4. This finding suggests that the T98D muta- tion alone was unable to confer binding selectivity for Arg at position —3, but rather changed the amino acid preference at the —4 position from a Pro to a Ser. However, one-third of the Ab! SH3 F91Y/T98D mutant selected phage that displayed an Arg at position —3. This mutation did not eliminate the selec- tion for Tyr at position —3, since at least one-half of the selected phage contained Tyr at this position. As with the T98D mutants, the selection for Pro at position —4 was lost in the double mutant. Therefore, the Ab] SH3 F91Y/T98D mu- tant has dual sequence preferences: one resembles the wild- type Abl SH3 sequence recognition motif, and the other shows a similar specificity for Arg in the —3 position.
