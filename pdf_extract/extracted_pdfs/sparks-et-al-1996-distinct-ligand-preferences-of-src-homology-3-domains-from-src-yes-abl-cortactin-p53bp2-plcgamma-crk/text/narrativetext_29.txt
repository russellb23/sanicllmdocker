PXXP Library Design and Screening. All known SH3 ligands contain at least one PXXP motif. We therefore de- signed the PXXP library to encode peptides of the form X¢6PXXPX¢. This bias results in a population of peptides highly enriched in SH3 ligands. In test screens of various phage- displayed peptide libraries with the Src SH3 domain, the PXXP library produced titers of SH3-binding phage ~100-fold greater than random peptide libraries of equivalent complexity (data not shown). With a complexity of 3 X 10° unique clones, the PXXP library has the capacity to encode all PXXP- containing 7-mers with 99% confidence, assuming a Poisson distribution of sequences within the library.
