SH3 domains from the closely related Src family members Src and Yes select peptides sharing similar consensus motifs: LXXRPLPXYP and YXXRPLPXLP, respectively. (In this and subsequent motifs, VY, ©, and + represent aliphatic, aromatic, and basic residues, respectively.)
