Despite the availability of both class I and II ligands in the PXXpP library and the ability of at least some SH3 domains to bind both class I and II ligands, we have found that each of the SH3 domains investigated in this work tends to select ligands that bind in one particular orientation. Whether this bias reflects the preferences of different SH3 domains for ligands that bind in a particular orientation or biases inherent in the phage selection process is not known.
