Several of the SH3-binding proteins possess proline-rich regions that do not conform to the consensus motifs of their respective SH3 targets, suggesting that factors other than the local structure of the ligand may contribute to the regulation of SH3-mediated protein-protein interactions in vivo. Such factors might include subcellular localization of SH3 or ligand proteins, cooperativity between different binding domains within a protein or complex of proteins, steric limitations upon the access of an SH3 domain to its ligands, and the existence of discontinuous SH3-binding regions in folded proteins.
