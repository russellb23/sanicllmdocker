However, not every IDR is sensitive to solution condition changes. We observed that some IDRs do not have a significant entropic force change, despite significant changes in their ensemble (Figure 6C,D). For example, M.Pvull displays a significant change in R,, but almost no change in entropic force (Figure 6C). On the other hand, the ensemble of the IDR of the regulator of nonsense transcripts 2 (hUpf2, Disprot ID: DP00949r013) is very sensitive to solution changes, and AS/kg changes accordingly.
