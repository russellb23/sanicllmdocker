We also show that the entropic force exerted by an IDR can change when the surrounding chemical environment changes. By modulating protein backbone:solvent interactions, we altered IDR ensembles and showed that the entropic force magnitude of most IDRs increased as their ensembles became more compact, validating the trend shown for different sequences (Figure 6B). This result also suggests the possibility of manipulating IDR entropic force by altering the physical— chemical composition of the cellular environment.*”*”
