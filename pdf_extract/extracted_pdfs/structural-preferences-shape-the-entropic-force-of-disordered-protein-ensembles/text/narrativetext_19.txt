We show that sequence-encoded structural preferences play an important role in determining the magnitude of this force: compact, spherical ensembles generate an entropic force that can be several times higher than more extended ensembles. We further show that changes in the surrounding solution’s chemistry can modulate the IDR entropic force strength. We propose that the entropic force is a sequence-dependent, environmentally tunable property of terminal IDR sequences.
