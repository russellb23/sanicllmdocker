Unlike well-folded proteins, IDR ensembles have a high conformational entropy. This conformational entropy can be reduced by covalently linking or tethering the IDR through one of its termini to a surface (Figure 1A). In this case, entropy is reduced due to the constraint placed upon the ensemble by the surface it is tethered to. As a result, upon tethering, an IDR will try to maximize its conformational entropy by producing an effective force that pulls up and away from the point of tethering, gaining entropy by increasing its number of accessible conformations generating an entropic force (Figure 1B).°°
