All-Atom Monte Carlo Simulation. All IDRs were simulated with the ABSINTH implicit solvent force field using the CAMPARI simulation suite v2_09052017.°° We chose the ABSINTH force field and the CAMPARI simulation suite because of their extensive benchmarking and their computational efficiency, allowing us to simulate sequences that are 30-100 residues long in 2—4 days on a single processor. The example parameter file and simulation settings are provided in the GitHub repository. Simulations were conducted at 310 K with 10’ steps of equilibration. After equilibration, conformations were written every 12,500 steps. For each IDR, we performed five independent simulations with ~5600 individual conformations in each repeat. This leads to a total of ~28,000 conformations for each IDR (details in Table S1). For PUMA scrambles, we performed three individual repeats, leading to ~16,800 conformations.
