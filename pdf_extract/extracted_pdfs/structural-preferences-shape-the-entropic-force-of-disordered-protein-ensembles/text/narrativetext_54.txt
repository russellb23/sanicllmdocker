Enhanced Sampling. In order to better understand the spatial relationship between the ensemble and the constraining surface, we perform several geometric transformations on each sampled conformation for calculating Qy: (1) To account for the possibility of stretching at the point of tethering, we vary the distance d between point A and S.. (2) To account for the possibility of rotation around the point of tethering, we vary the half-angle @ formed between the norm vector to S, with vector AB. (3) We rotate the vector AB with an angle ¢. All of the coordinates specified here are illustrated in Figure 1C. In total, we make 36 transformations (3 values for d, 3 values for 0, and 6 values for #) for each conformation of each simulated ensemble.
