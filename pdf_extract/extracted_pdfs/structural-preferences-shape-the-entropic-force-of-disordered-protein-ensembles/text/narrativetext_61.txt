Ensemble XZ-Projections. For each IDR conformation, we move A to the origin of the coordinate system and rotate the conformation to make AB fall on the Z-axis (Z > 0). The XZ- coordinate of each C, will provide an ensemble projection of the IDR ensemble on the XZ plane. The C, density was normalized by the number of amino acids in the sequence, the frame number of trajectories, and the bin size.
