This is because the SH3b-2 and SH3b-3 pair formed a stable structure unit through pairwise interactions between the distal loop of SH3b-2 and the RT loop of SH3b-3. On the other, SH3b-1 is isolated without a pairing domain. In all SH3b domains, the peptide ligand binding sites in an SH3e domain were occupied by their own RT-loop, indicating that this site did not act as the ligand-binding site. The surfaces of InIB SH3b domains are extremely basic, and the three targeting molecules of the SH3b domains of InIB, lipoteichoic acid, and heparin, and gClq-R are acidic. Electrostatic interactions between the SH3b domains and targeting molecules are important for binding, which explained the dissociable attachment to the bacterial cell wall through lipoteichoic acid molecules. However, specific binding to gClq-R could not be explained by electrostatic interactions alone, and the recognition mechanism for gClq-R remains unclear.
