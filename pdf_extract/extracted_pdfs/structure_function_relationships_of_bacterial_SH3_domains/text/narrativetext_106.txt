Marino et al. showed that binding to gClq-R and/or heparin required the release of InIB from the bacterial surface. Thus, they proposed that dissociable attachment to the bacterial surface via the SH3b domains permitted the localized release of InIB, and that the localized release of InlB may restrict the activation of Met to a small and localized area of the host cell, in order to couple InlB-induced host membrane dynamics with bacterial proximity during invasion (Marino et al. 2002).
