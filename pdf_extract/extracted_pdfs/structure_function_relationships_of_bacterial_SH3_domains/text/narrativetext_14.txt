A peptide ligand binds to a groove flanked by the RT loop and short 3)9 helix. The most conserved ligand binding residues in the eukaryote SH3 domains are Phe7, Phe9, Trp35, Pro49, and Tyr52, which form a hydrophobic groove to interact with Pro-rich peptide ligands (Fig. 1a).
