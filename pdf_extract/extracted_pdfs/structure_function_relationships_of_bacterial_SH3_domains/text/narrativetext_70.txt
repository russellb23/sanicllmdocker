The X-ray structures of y-d-glutamyl-l-diamino acid endopeptidases from Desulfovibrio vulgaris (3M1U) and Bacteroide thetaiotaomicrom WVP\-5482 (4ROK) have been found in PDB. The former structure has a SH3b domain with a long insertion in the RT-loop, and the latter structure has the tandem-repeated short SH3b domains (60 amino acids) without any significant insertions (Fig. Ic). The relative orientations and positions between the SH3b domains and catalytic domains are very similar to those of AVPCP and BcYkfc, respectively, which form the substrate-binding pocket between domains.
